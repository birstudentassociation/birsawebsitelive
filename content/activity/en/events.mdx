Different BIR year groups run events for each other.

## BIR the Future

A camp run by BIR freshmen for high-schoolers interested in joining BIR, usually running two days. It gives prospective students a look at BIR before they apply.

## BIR Open House

Run by sophomores, aimed mainly at high-schoolers considering BIR and their parents, to help them decide whether BIR is the right fit.

## BIR the Newbies

A one-to-two-day welcome event run by sophomores for incoming BIR freshmen, with activities for new students to get to know each other before the semester starts.

## BIR Byenior

A farewell event for graduating seniors, with live music from BIR students, a night party, and activities to close out the year. All year groups are welcome, not just graduating seniors.

## Other events through the year

BIR also runs BIR Movie Day, BIR Seminar and BIR Sports through the year.

Dates and sign-ups are announced on [News](/news) and BIRSA's socials.
