BIR has a rhythm of events that different year groups run for each other. Here are the flagship ones.

## BIR the Future

A camp run by BIR freshmen for high-schoolers interested in joining BIR. Think of it as a short summer camp, usually running two days, giving prospective students a taste of what BIR is like before they apply.

## BIR Open House

Run by sophomores, aimed mainly at high-schoolers considering BIR and their parents. It's about promoting the programme honestly and helping prospective students (and the people helping them decide) work out whether BIR is the right fit.

## BIR the Newbies

A one-to-two-day welcome event run by sophomores for the incoming BIR freshmen. The point is simple: get new students to actually know each other, through activities, before the semester gets busy.

## BIR Byenior

A farewell event for graduating seniors, with live music from BIR students, a night party, and activities to send the year off properly. All year groups are welcome, not just the seniors leaving.

## And more through the year

Beyond the flagship events, look out for BIR Movie Day, BIR Seminar, and BIR Sports throughout the year.

Dates and sign-ups for all of these are announced on [News](/news) and BIRSA's socials, so keep an eye there.
