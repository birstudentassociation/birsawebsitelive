## Contact form

The quickest way to reach the committee directly is our [contact form](/contact). Messages sent through the form go straight to the committee by email.

Social media carries event announcements and quick updates. Use email or the contact form for anything that needs a considered reply.

## Looking for the BIR programme office?

The details above are for BIRSA, the student association, not the university. For official academic matters (fees, registration, enrolment), contact the BIR programme office directly; see [The BIR programme](/activity/bir-programme) for its email, phone, address, and website. BIRSA is a student-run association, not a university office; see [What is BIRSA?](/activity/birsa) for how the two relate.
