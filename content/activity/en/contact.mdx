There are several ways to get in touch with BIRSA, depending on what's easiest for you.

## Contact form

The quickest way to reach the committee directly is our [contact form](/contact). Messages sent through the form go straight to the committee by email.

## Email and socials

- **Email:** <Email address="birsa@tu.ac.th" />
- **Instagram:** [@student_birsa](https://www.instagram.com/student_birsa/)
- **Facebook:** [BIR Student Association](https://www.facebook.com/BIRStudentAssociation/)

Social media is generally the fastest way to catch event announcements and quick updates, while email and the contact form are better for anything that needs a considered reply.

## Looking for the BIR programme office?

The details above are for BIRSA, the student association — not the university. For official academic matters (fees, registration, enrolment), contact the BIR programme office directly; see [The BIR programme](/activity/bir-programme) for its email, phone, address, and website. BIRSA is student-run and unofficial; see [What is BIRSA?](/activity/birsa) for how the two relate.
