BIRSA is not the only elected student body BIR students can run for. There are bodies at four levels: your programme, your faculty, your campus and the university as a whole.

| Level                                         | Bodies you can run for                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| BIR (programme level)                         | BIRSA (the BIR Student Association) and BIR class councils                             |
| Singhadang (the Faculty of Political Science) | The Political Science Students' Committee and class councils                           |
| TPC (Tha Prachan Campus)                      | TUSU TPC and TUSC TPC, the campus branches of the university student union and council |
| TU (Thammasat University, all campuses)       | TUSU, TUSC, and ECTU                                                                   |

TUSU is the Thammasat University Student Union, TUSC is the Thammasat University Student Council, and ECTU is the Election Commission of Thammasat University. Each has its own scope and processes at the university level.

Running for any of these means representing BIR students beyond BIRSA. See [Current officers](/activity/roles) for what BIRSA's own committee positions involve, or [Getting involved](/student-life/getting-involved/student-bodies) for other ways to get started.
