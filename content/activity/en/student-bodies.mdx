Did you know you can run for any of these? BIRSA isn't the only elected student body you're eligible for. There's a whole ladder of elected student bodies open to BIR students, at four levels: your programme, your faculty, your campus, and the university as a whole.

| Level                                         | Bodies you can run for                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| BIR (programme level)                         | BIRSA (the BIR Student Association) and BIR class councils                             |
| Singhadang (the Faculty of Political Science) | The Political Science Students' Committee and class councils                           |
| TPC (Tha Prachan Campus)                      | TUSU TPC and TUSC TPC, the campus branches of the university student union and council |
| TU (Thammasat University, all campuses)       | TUSU, TUSC, and ECTU                                                                   |

A few of those acronyms are worth knowing generally: TUSU is the Thammasat University Student Union, TUSC is the Thammasat University Student Council, and ECTU is the Election Commission of Thammasat University. Each has its own scope and processes at the university level, so if you're seriously considering running for one, it's worth looking into how that specific body works before you commit.

Whichever level interests you, running for any of these is a real way to represent BIR students beyond BIRSA itself. See [Officer roles](/activity/roles) for what BIRSA's own committee positions involve, or [Getting involved](/student-life/home/getting-involved) for other ways to get started.
