BIRSA (the BIR Student Association) is the student-run association for students of the Bachelor of Political Science in Politics and International Relations (BIR) at Thammasat University's Faculty of Political Science.

## Our mission

We exist to make student life at BIR better, socially, practically, and in how students are represented. That means running events, maintaining this portal as a clear source of information, and giving students a voice in matters that affect their time at BIR.

BIRSA is a student-run association that works alongside the BIR programme and faculty, but we are not a university office. This website is BIRSA's official site, though it is not an official university channel. For official academic and enrolment matters, always use the BIR Programme's own channels. See [The BIR programme](/activity/bir-programme).

## The committee

BIRSA is run by an elected committee of BIR students: officers who each lead one area of BIRSA's work, and assistant officers who support them day to day. See [Officer roles](/activity/roles) for the full roster and what each position is responsible for.

Committee decisions are generally made collectively, with members discussing proposals relevant to their area before larger decisions are agreed as a group. Bigger initiatives, such as new events or major changes to student services, typically involve wider consultation with BIR students, not just the committee alone.

## How to join

Committee positions typically open up around the start of each academic year, with an announcement made through BIRSA's social channels and [News](/news). If you're interested in getting involved before a formal opening, reaching out to a current committee member is a reasonable first step; see [Contact BIRSA](/contact).

You do not need to be an existing committee member to contribute; many BIRSA activities also rely on volunteers and short-term helpers around specific events. See [Getting involved](/student-life/home/getting-involved) for more ways in.
