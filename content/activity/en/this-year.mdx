<Notice variant="info" title="This year's mission">
  Keep what's good, bring what's needed, and fix what's not working.
</Notice>

## What we're keeping

We're following up on the Common Room renovation project until it is finished. We're keeping the BIR website and Discord server running as the places to find information and talk to each other. We're improving Movie Days by letting students vote on which films get shown, instead of the committee picking alone. We're building on last year's work simplifying the "Make Your Club" process.

## What we're bringing

A few things are new this year. We're introducing a Seminar and Book Fair. We're running Wellness Days during exam season. We're setting up new fundraising programmes so sports teams and clubs get more resources, instead of a thin budget stretched across everyone.

## Backing every club

BIRSA fully supports every sports club at BIR, from planning activities to managing budgets. We also make sure clubs have enough equipment for everyone who wants to take part, not just the first few to sign up.

## BIR festive activities

Throughout the year, we run campus events that bring students together outside class, from a Halloween party to Valentine's events.

Follow [News](/news) for dates and sign-ups as they're announced, or reach out through [Contact BIRSA](/contact) if you have questions.
