<Notice variant="info" title="This year's mission">
  Keep what's good, bring what's needed, and fix what's not working. That's the frame this year's
  committee is using for everything below.
</Notice>

## What we're keeping

Some things are working, so we're continuing them rather than reinventing them. We're following up on the Common Room renovation project until it's actually finished, not just started. We're keeping the BIR website and Discord server running as the places to find information and talk to each other. We're improving Movie Days by letting students vote on which films get shown, instead of the committee picking alone. And we're building on last year's work simplifying the "Make Your Club" process, so starting a new club stays straightforward.

## What we're bringing

A few things are new this year. We're introducing a Seminar & Book Fair. We're running Wellness Days during exam season, because exams are stressful enough without ignoring it. And we're setting up new fundraising programmes so sports teams and clubs get the resources they deserve, instead of stretching a thin budget across everyone.

## Backing every club

BIRSA fully supports every sports club at BIR, from planning activities to managing budgets. We also make sure clubs have enough equipment for everyone who wants to take part, not just the first few to sign up.

## BIR festive activities

Throughout the year, we run colourful campus moments that give students a reason to come together outside class, from a Halloween party to Valentine's events. They're not just for fun on the day; they're the memories you look back on later.

Follow [News](/news) for dates and sign-ups as they're announced, or reach out through [Contact BIRSA](/contact) if you have questions.
