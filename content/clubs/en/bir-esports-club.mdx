BIR Esports Club is a community for BIR students to come together and connect through gaming. Members share news, exchange ideas, build friendships, and get better at the games they play.

The club is open to casual and competitive players. It also develops competitive players with support and guidance from senior members, so that BIR is represented at bigger competitions: the TPC Games, the National University Games, and the Thailand University Esports Championship.

## Record

At last year's TPC Games:

| Title                | Finish       |
| -------------------- | ------------ |
| ROV (Arena of Valor) | First place  |
| Valorant             | Second place |
| EA Sports FC Online  | Second place |

Beyond Tha Prachan, club members have played for Thammasat at the National University Games and the Thailand University Esports Championship.

## This year

- Compete in the TPC Games
- Compete in external events
- Grow the club, and run some fun activities along the way

The club's stated goal is first place in every competition.

## How to join

Follow [@biresport.club](https://www.instagram.com/biresport.club/) on Instagram, where the LINE and Discord group links, tournaments, and member sign-ups are posted. All skill levels are welcome, whatever you play.
