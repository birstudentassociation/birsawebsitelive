BIR Football gets students out from behind their laptops for a game. The club plays eleven-a-side, mixing casual kickabouts with practice sessions and friendly matches against other programmes, faculties, and universities.

Sessions run on both real grass and artificial turf, depending on where the club is playing that week.

## A word from the club

If we had to sum up our club in one word, it would be "family". We care most about having fun and building togetherness, carrying forward the warm community culture that Singh Daeng, the Red Lion, is known for.

Training is relaxed and unpressured. We usually head out for good food together afterwards, which is where a lot of the good vibes happen, and it is a great way to build connections across year groups.

One more thing worth knowing: we are not just a men's team, we have a women's team too. And if playing is not your thing but you would still like to be part of it, we would love to have you on board behind the scenes. You do not need any football experience, just an open mind, a willingness to learn, and a readiness to have fun together. See you on the pitch.

## How to join

Follow [@birfootballclub](https://www.instagram.com/birfootballclub/) on Instagram, where match days, the LINE group link, and new-player sign-ups are posted. There are no trials and no experience is required: bring trainers and water and turn up.
