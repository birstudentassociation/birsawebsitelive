BIR Music Club describes itself as the biggest music club in BIR. It brings a fun atmosphere to an academic setting.

The club is open to instrumentalists, singers, producers, and anyone who wants to jam between classes.

## What the club has done

The club plays the big dates in the BIR and faculty calendar, including BIR Byenior, SD Crazy Week, and SDSD 2025: stage sets, outdoor shows, and the late nights that follow them.

## Auditions

The club auditions for a full band line-up:

- Guitar
- Vocals
- Bass
- Keyboard
- Drums

All levels are welcome. You do not need to read music or own expensive gear to take part.

## How to join

Follow [@birmusicclub](https://www.instagram.com/birmusicclub/) on Instagram. Auditions are announced there, along with the link to the audition group, and new members are usually welcomed at the start of each semester.
