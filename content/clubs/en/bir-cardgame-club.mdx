BIR CardGame Club plays card games and talks about them. It meets people across year groups and does not run to a fixed venue. The club plays wherever there is a table.

## What gets played

Members bring decks for whatever they are into, including:

- Cardfight!! Vanguard
- Magic: The Gathering
- Yu-Gi-Oh!
- Duel Masters
- Union Arena

New games and deck types get added as they come along. The club also plays tabletop games, not only trading card games. Members have also played in outside tournaments, up to regional championship level.

<Notice variant="info">
  Members bring games to teach. You do not need a deck or knowledge of the rules to join.
</Notice>

## How to join

Follow [@bircardgame](https://www.instagram.com/bircardgame/) on Instagram, where the LINE group and Discord links are posted. Sign-ups are announced at the start of each semester.
