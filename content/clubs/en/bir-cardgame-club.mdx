BIR CardGame Club describes what it does in three steps: play card games, play card games together (sometimes), and talk about card games (oftentimes). That is roughly accurate, and the third one takes up more time than the first two.

It is a relaxed way to meet people across year groups, and it does not run to a fixed venue. The club plays wherever there is a table.

## What gets played

Members bring decks for whatever they are into, including:

- Cardfight!! Vanguard
- Magic: The Gathering
- Yu-Gi-Oh!
- Duel Masters
- Union Arena

New games and new deck-building get picked up as they come along, and it is not only trading card games: tabletop games get a look in too. Members have also played in outside tournaments, up to regional championship level.

<Notice variant="info">
  Beginner? Even better. Members bring games to teach, and nobody expects you to arrive with a deck
  or know the rules.
</Notice>

## How to join

Follow [@bircardgame](https://www.instagram.com/bircardgame/) on Instagram, where the LINE group and Discord links are posted. Sign-ups are announced at the start of each semester.
