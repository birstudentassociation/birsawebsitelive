Kien is a writers' club. It publishes written work by Thammasat students with a focus on the humanities, so what appears is not limited to BIR or to politics.

## How the club is organised

There are three roles, and members can hold more than one:

- **Writers** pitch and write the pieces
- **Editors** work with writers to sharpen drafts before publication
- **Graphic designers** give each piece its look on the page

The club publishes on a steady rhythm: articles every two weeks, and poetry once a month.

## What Kien publishes

Recent work gives a fair sense of the range:

| Piece                                                 | What it is                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| _The Salt of the Earth_                               | A poem about potash mining in Dan Khun Thot, published with photography  |
| _Thai Foreign Relations during Thaksin's Premiership_ | A piece of political analysis                                            |
| _A Body, a Burden_                                    | An analysis of female bodily autonomy in Claudia Piñeiro's _Elena Knows_ |

## How to join

Follow [@kien.club](https://www.instagram.com/kien.club/) on Instagram. The club recruits for all three roles and posts the application details there, along with everything it publishes.
