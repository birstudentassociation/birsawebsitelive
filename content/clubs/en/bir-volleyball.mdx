BIR Volleyball runs casual sessions and friendly matches for anyone who wants to play, whatever your level.

Sessions keep members active during term and are open to anyone learning the game for the first time. Bring water and shoes you can move in. No experience is needed to join a session.

## How to join

Sessions and new players are announced through BIRSA at the start of each semester. Watch BIRSA's Instagram at [@student_birsa](https://www.instagram.com/student_birsa/), or ask at BIRSA's welcome events.
