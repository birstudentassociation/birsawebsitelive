BIR Basketball Club is a community for students interested in basketball, from complete beginners to competitive players. Members play, connect, and improve together through the sport.

## The plan

- **Regular sessions.** The club plays at the TU Tha Prachan gymnasium and at B-Pro Pata Pinklao.
- **A team to build.** Those sessions are also preparation for next year's Tha Prachan Games.
- **Open to everyone.** Beginners, casual players, and competitive players are welcome.

The club held its first meet in May 2026 and has been playing since.

## How to join

Follow [@bir.basketballclub](https://www.instagram.com/bir.basketballclub/) on Instagram, where session times, the LINE group link, and new-player sign-ups are posted. Turn up in trainers, bring water, and get on the court.
