Inter-Portfolio 2 and TCAS 3 need one English test result at or above the minimum score. Inter-Portfolio 1 and the two non-Thai rounds need an official SAT result on Evidence-Based Reading and Writing, with no minimum score. Every result must be no more than 2 years old on the day you apply.

<Notice variant="info" title="The BIR website is the official source">

The BIR office publishes the official requirements at [birpolsci.com](https://www.birpolsci.com/prospectivestudents). Scores and rules can change, so check that page before you apply.

</Notice>

## Minimum English scores

You need one of these results. You do not need more than one.

<EnglishTestScores />

You must hold a valid result that meets the minimum on the day you apply. You cannot send your score later. The result must be no more than 2 years old on that day.

## Which rounds need which test

| Round                        | English evidence you need                                                        |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inter-Portfolio 1            | Official SAT result on Evidence-Based Reading and Writing, with no minimum score |
| Inter-Portfolio 2            | One test from the table above                                                    |
| TCAS 3                       | One test from the table above                                                    |
| Non-Thai round 1 and round 2 | Official SAT result on Evidence-Based Reading and Writing, with no minimum score |

For Inter-Portfolio 1 and the non-Thai rounds, the BIR committee calls the applicants with the highest SAT scores to interview.

## School qualifications

How you apply depends on how your school records are graded.

### Thai school records

If your school gives a GPAX on a 4.0 scale, use your transcript and your ปพ.1. The minimum grades differ by round.

| Round                        | School record you need                                                             |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inter-Portfolio 1            | In Mathayom 6 with at least 4 semesters of results, or graduated. No minimum GPAX. |
| Inter-Portfolio 2            | In Mathayom 6 with a cumulative GPAX over 5 semesters of at least 3.75             |
| TCAS 3                       | A complete GPAX of at least 2.80 over 6 semesters, or an equivalent                |
| Non-Thai round 1 and round 2 | In Grade 12 with at least 4 semesters of results, or graduated. No minimum GPAX.   |

### GED diploma

The minimum is 165 in each subject. A total above 660 does not help if any one subject is below 165. You must have finished all subject tests and hold the official GED Official Transcript and High School Equivalency Credential Certificate. The BIR office does not accept scores that are still pending when the round closes.

| Round                          | What to send                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inter-Portfolio 1 and non-Thai | The HSCES High School Equivalence Certificate and GPAX Equivalence Certificate                       |
| TCAS 3                         | Your GED diploma and your GED score report. The BIR programme does not accept the HSCES certificate. |

### International school and overseas records

If your school records are not on a 4.0 scale, for example an international school in Thailand or abroad, GCE O Level, GCSE, IGCSE or the International Baccalaureate, apply with two certificates from the High School Certificate Equivalency system (HSCES).

- the High School Equivalence Certificate
- the GPAX Equivalence Certificate

HSCES is run by the Academic Testing Center at Chulalongkorn University. Use the [HSCES website](https://hsces.atc.chula.ac.th/) and search for BIR. The programme is listed as 10050103903201E, the Bachelor of Political Science in Politics and International Relations (International Program), Faculty of Political Science. HSCES completes the process about 7 working days after it confirms your payment, so start early.

The [High School Certificate Equivalency guide (PDF, English)](https://image.makewebcdn.com/makeweb/0/fAusajSlU/Document/High_School_Certificate_Equivalency.pdf) shows how GED and foreign diploma applicants apply.

### TCAS 3 qualifications from other systems

For TCAS 3, these also qualify.

- GCE O Level, GCSE or IGCSE with at least grade C in five different subjects, plus AS at least grade A to E in five subjects, or GCE A Level at least grade A to E in three subjects
- International Baccalaureate with at least 4 in five different subjects

### Exchange students

If you took a high school exchange year abroad, you must have a pass in all subjects. Thai students submit the ปพ.1 from their Thai school. Read [Common questions](/information/applying-to-bir/common-questions).
