You upload scans of your documents with your application. You certify each copy yourself by signing and dating it. Which documents you need depends on your round.

<Notice variant="info" title="The BIR website is the official source">

The BIR office publishes the official document lists at [birpolsci.com](https://www.birpolsci.com/prospectivestudents). Requirements can change, so check the page for your round before you apply.

</Notice>

## Inter-Portfolio 1

- A certified copy of your National ID Card
- A certified copy of your high school transcript
- Your high school GPAX with at least 4 semesters (ปพ.1), or the HSCES High School Equivalence Certificate and GPAX Equivalence Certificate
- A certified copy of your high school student certificate. If you are studying, send the Certificate of Student Status (ปพ.7). If you have finished, send the high school diploma (ปพ.2).
- A certified copy of your official SAT score report
- An ID photo

You do not need a portfolio.

## Inter-Portfolio 2

- The BIR Inter-Portfolio template as a PDF of no more than 10 MB
- A certified copy of your National ID Card
- A certified copy of your high school transcript
- A certified copy of your Certificate of Student Status (ปพ.7)
- A certified copy of your English proficiency test score
- An ID photo

The [BIR Inter-Portfolio template](https://image.makewebcdn.com/makeweb/0/fAusajSlU/Document/BIR2027_Interportfolio_Form_.docx) is a Word file. Fill it in, export it as a PDF and upload it. Read [Apply through Inter-Portfolio 2](/information/applying-to-bir/inter-portfolio-2) for what it contains.

## TCAS 3 and the BIR written exam

- A certified copy of your National ID Card
- A certified copy of your high school transcript, or the GPAX Equivalence Certificate from HSCES
- A certified copy of your student certificate, or the High School Equivalence Certificate from HSCES. It must show that you study in Mathayom 6 and expect to graduate in academic year 2026 (ปีการศึกษา 2569), or that you are in the last year of high school.
- A certified copy of your English proficiency test score
- An ID photo

If you hold a GED, send your GED diploma and GED score report instead of the HSCES certificates. Read [English tests and school qualifications](/information/applying-to-bir/english-tests-and-qualifications).

## Non-Thai rounds 1 and 2

- A copy of your passport
- A colour ID photo, 4 cm by 3 cm, with no hat and a plain background
- Your official high school transcript, or the HSCES certificates if your records are not on a 4.0 scale
- A certified copy of your official SAT score report
- A curriculum vitae or resume
- A statement of purpose of 500 to 1,000 words
- The bank payment slip for the application fee of 1,000 baht or 35 US dollars

If your school does not issue records in Thai or English, send the original records and a literal, complete English translation. Read [Apply if you are not Thai](/information/applying-to-bir/non-thai-applicants).

## ID photo

Upload your ID photo as a PDF, JPEG or PNG file of no more than 20 MB. Non-Thai applicants use a colour photo, 4 cm by 3 cm, with no hat and a plain background.

## How to certify copies

Certify every copy yourself before you scan it.

1. Make a clear copy of the original document.
2. Write your signature on each page.
3. Write the date next to your signature.
4. Certify your ปพ.1 transcript on both the front and the back. Scan both sides.
5. Certify high school diplomas and English test scores in the same way.
6. Check that every scan is clear and legible.

The BIR office shows examples in the [Sample of certified documents (PDF)](https://image.makewebcdn.com/makeweb/0/fAusajSlU/Document/SAMPLE_Certified_DOCs.pdf). It includes a certified National ID Card, passport, high school transcripts and diplomas, and English test scores. Each carries the stamp "Certified by Applicant's Signature" and the date.

## Documents in other languages

You can send documents in Thai or English. If a document is in another language, send the original and a literal, complete English translation.

## Originals at the interview

For Inter-Portfolio 1 and 2, bring the originals of your ID card or passport, your student certificate, your transcript and your test report to the interview. The interview takes place at the Faculty of Political Science, Thammasat University (Tha Prachan).

## After you apply on myTCAS

You do not take your documents to the faculty in person after you apply on myTCAS. You send them online, through a Google Form or by email. Read [Common questions](/information/applying-to-bir/common-questions).
