If you are not Thai, you apply in one of two rounds. Round 1 has 3 seats and closes on 6 November 2026. Round 2 has 2 seats and closes on 24 January 2027. You apply with a Google Form, an SAT Evidence-Based Reading and Writing result and an application fee of 1,000 baht or 35 US dollars. There is no minimum GPAX.

<Notice variant="info" title="The BIR website is the official source">

The BIR office publishes the official information at [birpolsci.com](https://www.birpolsci.com/trackoutside). Dates and requirements can change, so check that page before you apply. The official announcement is in Thai only. The BIR office is preparing an English translation.

</Notice>

## Who can apply

You can apply if you

- do not hold Thai nationality and are not a dual national with a Thai passport
- are studying Grade 12 with records covering at least 4 semesters, or have graduated from Grade 12 or an equivalent
- have an official SAT result on Evidence-Based Reading and Writing, taken within 2 years before the date you apply

If you live in Thailand, you must be legally resident and hold valid government ID.

If your school records are not on a 4.0 GPAX scale, for example a GED, GCE O Level, GCSE, IGCSE or International Baccalaureate, apply with the HSCES High School Equivalence Certificate and GPAX Equivalence Certificate. You can get them from the [High School Certificate Equivalency system](https://hsces.atc.chula.ac.th/). Read [English tests and school qualifications](/information/applying-to-bir/english-tests-and-qualifications).

The [official announcement for international applicants](https://image.makewebcdn.com/makeweb/0/fAusajSlU/Document/_Signed__%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A8%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%A3_BIR_Non_Thai_70.pdf) lists the full qualifications. ## How you are chosen

1. The BIR office ranks applicants by their SAT Evidence-Based Reading and Writing score.
2. Only the applicants with the best scores are called to an interview.
3. The BIR committee interviews them online, in English. The committee's decision is final.

If you do not pass the interview, the committee does not consider your SAT score.

## How to apply

1. Complete the [online application form](https://forms.gle/gArKnM6CQyR6xwy6A). It opens at 09:00 on 6 October 2026, Bangkok time. Round 1 closes at 23:59 on 6 November 2026. Round 2 uses the same form and closes on 24 January 2027.
2. Pay the application fee and keep your bank payment slip.
3. Go to your online interview with the BIR committee.
4. Complete your enrolment confirmation if you are successful.

You must pay and send your proof of payment by the closing date of your round, or your application is void. The BIR office cannot return the documents you submit.

## Pay the application fee

The fee is 1,000 baht or 35 US dollars. It covers all admission and examination costs for the BIR admission procedure. It is not refundable. You pay any international bank transfer charges on top.

## Documents

Send these with your application.

- A copy of your passport
- A colour ID photo, 4 cm by 3 cm, with no hat and a plain background
- Your official high school transcript. If the records are not on a 4.0 scale, send the HSCES certificates.
- A certified copy of your official SAT score report
- A curriculum vitae or resume
- A statement of purpose of 500 to 1,000 words
- The bank payment slip for the application fee

If your school does not issue records in Thai or English, send the original records and a literal, complete English translation.

Read [Documents you need](/information/applying-to-bir/documents) for how to certify copies.
