These answers come from the BIR office's frequently asked questions page. The 2.80 GPAX in the first answer applies to TCAS 3.

<Notice variant="info" title="The BIR website is the official source">

The BIR office publishes the official answers at [birpolsci.com](https://www.birpolsci.com/qanda). Rules can change, so check that page before you apply.

</Notice>

## My school cannot issue all 6 semesters of grades in time. What can I do?

This applies to TCAS 3. You have two ways to show your grades.

1. During the application period, send your grades for 5 semesters, with a minimum GPA of 2.80, and your student status certificate (ปพ.7).
2. By the interview date, send your ปพ.1 showing your graduation date and a GPA of not less than 2.80.

If you meet neither condition, the programme may reject your application.

## I did a one year exchange programme. Which documents do I send?

Send the document issued by your Thai school. The programme considers that document. If you took the exchange year in high school, you must have a pass in all subjects.

## Can I send documents in Thai?

Yes. The BIR office accepts documents in Thai or English.

## How do I confirm my place if I am successful?

Confirm on [myTCAS](https://www.mytcas.com), following the TCAS round for your admission round. Inter-Portfolio 1 and 2 are joint direct admission rounds of Thammasat University's international programmes. They follow the university calendar. You confirm with TCAS in the Portfolio round for Inter-Portfolio 1 and the Quota round for Inter-Portfolio 2.

You must not have confirmed your right to a place in any other round.

## Do I take my documents to the faculty after I apply on myTCAS?

No. You send them online. The English FAQ says to use a Google Form or email. The Thai FAQ says to use a Google Form. If you are unsure, ask the BIR office.

## Can I send my English test score after I apply?

No. You need a valid score that meets the criteria on the day you apply. It must be no more than 2 years old. Read [English tests and school qualifications](/services/applying-to-bir/english-tests-and-qualifications).

## My GED total is above 660 but one subject is below 165. Can I apply?

No. The minimum is 165 in each subject. If any subject is below 165, you do not meet the criteria.

## My GED results are not ready by the deadline. Can I send the scores I have?

No. You must have finished all the subject tests and hold the official certification. This is the GED Official Transcript and the High School Equivalency Credential Certificate.
