BIR admission for 2027 entry runs from October 2026 to May 2027. The table below lists every date in order, by month. The BIR office announces some dates later.

<Notice variant="info" title="The BIR website is the official source">

The BIR office publishes the official dates at [birpolsci.com](https://www.birpolsci.com/prospectivestudents). Dates can change, so check that page before you apply.

</Notice>

## Dates that are not fixed

- The TCAS 3 interview and the announcement of enrolment are to be announced.
- The TCAS 3 admission period on 12 to 13 May 2027 is to be confirmed.

## Register on myTCAS first

TCAS 2027 applicants register on [myTCAS](https://www.mytcas.com). Registration for the 2027 cycle opened on 15 July 2026. Successful applicants confirm their place on myTCAS in the period set for their round. If you confirm in one round, you cannot apply in a later round.
