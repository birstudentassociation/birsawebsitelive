It costs 1,000 baht to apply for Inter-Portfolio 1 or 2, and 500 baht to sit the BIR written exam. Applicants who are not Thai pay 1,000 baht or 35 US dollars. The application fees are not refundable. Tuition fees are in the student life guide.

<Notice variant="info" title="The BIR website is the official source">

The BIR office publishes the official information at [birpolsci.com](https://www.birpolsci.com/prospectivestudents). Fees can change, so check that page before you apply. The BIR admission pages for Thai applicants do not state the application fees. The fees on this page come from the official Thai announcements and the BIR application guide.

</Notice>

## Application fees

| Round                        | Fee                         | How to pay                                               |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inter-Portfolio 1            | 1,000 baht                  | At a Bangkok Bank counter, or by QR code in any bank app |
| Inter-Portfolio 2            | 1,000 baht                  | At a Bangkok Bank counter, or by QR code in any bank app |
| BIR written exam (TCAS 3)    | 500 baht                    | At a bank counter, or by QR code in a mobile banking app |
| Non-Thai round 1 and round 2 | 1,000 baht or 35 US dollars | By bank transfer to Kasikorn Bank                        |

The application fee for Inter-Portfolio 1 and 2 is not refundable, and the BIR office cannot return the documents you submit.

For Inter-Portfolio 1, pay by 23:59 on 7 November 2026. Read [Use the application website](/information/applying-to-bir/application-website) for how to pay.

### Bank details for applicants who are not Thai

| Detail         | Value         |
| -------------- | ------------- |
| Bank           | Kasikorn Bank |
| Account name   | BIR Program   |
| Account number | 645-1-00017-7 |
| SWIFT code     | KASITHBK      |

You pay any overseas bank charges on top of the fee. You must pay and send your proof of payment by the closing date of your round, or your application is void. Read [Apply if you are not Thai](/information/applying-to-bir/non-thai-applicants).

## Tuition

The BIR admission pages do not list tuition fees. Read [Tuition, fees and refunds](/student-life/money/tuition-and-fees). Tuition follows the Thammasat University fee regulation for special programmes (B.E. 2553) and the Faculty of Political Science announcement on BIR fees (B.E. 2562).

## Scholarships

The BIR scholarship page lists two places to ask. It does not give amounts, deadlines or criteria.

| Scholarship                 | Who to contact                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Thammasat Scholarships      | Student Affairs Division, Thammasat University. See the [Thammasat Scholarships Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/thammasat.scholarships). |
| International opportunities | Office of International Affairs, Thammasat University. See the [Office of International Affairs website](https://oia.tu.ac.th/).                 |

The Thammasat International Student Recruitment Program (TISR) is an admission and scholarship programme for international students who apply to Thammasat University. Email <Email address="info.inter@tu.ac.th" /> or visit the [Office of International Affairs website](https://oia.tu.ac.th/).

Read [Scholarships, loans and hardship help](/student-life/money/financial-help) for other sources of money once you are a student.
