Inter-Portfolio 1 is the first BIR round for Thai applicants. It has 45 seats. You apply from 6 October to 6 November 2026, and the BIR committee interviews the applicants with the highest SAT scores. There is no minimum GPAX, and you do not need a portfolio.

<Notice variant="info" title="The BIR website is the official source">

The BIR office publishes the official information at [birpolsci.com](https://www.birpolsci.com/interportfolio1). Dates and requirements can change, so check that page before you apply.

</Notice>

If you are not Thai, read [Apply if you are not Thai](/information/applying-to-bir/non-thai-applicants).

## Who can apply

You can apply if you

- are studying Grade 12 (Mathayom 6) and have results for at least 4 semesters, or have graduated from Grade 12 or an equivalent
- have an official SAT result on Evidence-Based Reading and Writing, taken within 2 years before the date you apply
- have never confirmed your right to a place in the TCAS Clearing House system

If you studied in an international school whose grades are not on a 4.0 scale, hold a GED diploma, or studied at a high school overseas, apply with both certificates from the High School Equivalence Certificate system (HSCES). These are the High School Equivalence Certificate and the GPAX Equivalence Certificate. Read [English tests and school qualifications](/information/applying-to-bir/english-tests-and-qualifications) for how to get them.

If you took a high school exchange year abroad, you must have a pass in all subjects.

There is no minimum GPAX. The BIR office does not ask for a portfolio.

## How you are chosen

1. The BIR office ranks applicants by their SAT Evidence-Based Reading and Writing score.
2. Only the applicants with the best scores are called to an interview.
3. The BIR committee interviews them. The committee's decision is final.

The BIR office can refuse an unqualified applicant on the interview day.

## How to apply

1. Register on [myTCAS](https://www.mytcas.com).
2. Apply for Inter-Portfolio 1 on the BIR admission website. The application window runs from 09:00 on 6 October to 23:59 on 6 November 2026, Bangkok time. Follow [Use the application website](/information/applying-to-bir/application-website).
3. Pay the application fee of 1,000 baht by 23:59 on 7 November 2026.
4. Go to the interview at the Faculty of Political Science, Thammasat University (Tha Prachan), on 15 November 2026.
5. Confirm your place on myTCAS on 10 and 11 March 2027, in TCAS Round 1 (Portfolio).

The BIR office announces who is called to interview on 13 November 2026 and who is successful on 15 December 2026.

## Documents

Upload these documents with your application.

- A certified copy of your National ID Card
- A certified copy of your high school transcript
- Your high school GPAX with at least 4 semesters (ปพ.1), or the HSCES High School Equivalence Certificate and GPAX Equivalence Certificate
- A certified copy of your high school student certificate. If you are still studying, this is the Certificate of Student Status (ปพ.7). If you have finished, it is the high school diploma (ปพ.2).
- A certified copy of your official SAT score report
- An ID photo as a PDF, JPEG or PNG file of no more than 20 MB

Bring the originals of your ID card, student certificate, transcript and SAT report to the interview. Read [Documents you need](/information/applying-to-bir/documents) for the checklist and how to certify copies.
