TCAS 3 is the third BIR round, and it is for Thai applicants only. It has 15 seats, plus any seats that other rounds do not fill. You first sit the BIR written exam, then use your score to apply on myTCAS, then go to an interview.

<Notice variant="info" title="The BIR website is the official source">

The BIR office publishes the official information at [birpolsci.com](https://www.birpolsci.com/tcas3admission). Dates and requirements can change, so check that page before you apply.

</Notice>

<AdmissionDates round="tcas-3" />

## Who can apply

You can apply if you

- hold one of the high school qualifications below
- have one English test result from the last 2 years that meets the minimum score
- have never confirmed your right to a place in the TCAS Clearing House system

You qualify with any one of these.

| Qualification                                  | What you need                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Thai high school (Mathayom 4 to 6)             | A complete GPAX of at least 2.80 over 6 semesters, or an equivalent                                                                                 |
| GED diploma                                    | At least 165 in each subject                                                                                                                        |
| GCE O Level, GCSE or IGCSE, with AS or A Level | At least grade C in five different subjects, plus AS at least grade A to E in five subjects, or GCE A Level at least grade A to E in three subjects |
| International Baccalaureate                    | At least 4 in five different subjects                                                                                                               |

If your records are not on a 4.0 scale, for example from an international school or an overseas school, apply with the HSCES High School Equivalence Certificate and GPAX Equivalence Certificate. A GED is different. Apply with your GED diploma and your GED score report. The BIR programme does not accept the HSCES certificate for a GED in TCAS 3.

If your school cannot issue all 6 semesters of grades in time, read [Common questions](/services/applying-to-bir/common-questions).

These are the minimum English scores. You need one test only.

## The written exam

The exam is 100 percent of your selection score.

| Part                                                                       | Weight     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| General knowledge in politics and international relations, multiple choice | 40 percent |
| English essay on politics and international relations, writing             | 60 percent |

The exam is from 10:00 to 12:00 on Sunday 21 March 2027 at the Faculty of Political Science, Thammasat University (Tha Prachan). The fee is 500 baht. Thammasat University publishes its written exam rules in the [official TU written examination announcement](https://www.tuadmissions.in.th/img/2026082707354922.pdf). It is in Thai and covers the exam day rules, including the ID you must bring and what you may take into the room.

BIR applicants apply for the exam on the BIR admission website, not on the Thammasat University admissions website.

## How to apply

1. Create an account and apply for the BIR written exam on the BIR admission website between 2 and 17 February 2027. Follow [Use the application website](/services/applying-to-bir/application-website).
2. Pay the exam fee of 500 baht.
3. Check on 11 March 2027 that the BIR office lists you as eligible for the exam, and find your seat.
4. Sit the exam on 21 March 2027.
5. Wait for your score on 22 April 2027.
6. Register on [myTCAS](https://www.mytcas.com) and apply for BIR in TCAS Round 3 (Admission) from 7 to 11 May 2027. Your BIR written exam score is required.
7. Go to the interview at the Faculty of Political Science. The BIR office has not yet announced the date.
8. Confirm your place on myTCAS. TCAS announces the first results on 22 May 2027, and you confirm on 22 and 23 May 2027. TCAS announces a second round of results on 27 May 2027, and confirmation is automatic.

## Documents

Upload these documents with your application.

- A certified copy of your National ID Card
- A certified copy of your high school transcript, or the GPAX Equivalence Certificate from HSCES
- A certified copy of your student certificate, or the High School Equivalence Certificate from HSCES. The certificate must show that you study in Mathayom 6 and expect to graduate in academic year 2026 (ปีการศึกษา 2569), or that you are in the last year of high school.
- A certified copy of your English proficiency test score
- An ID photo as a PDF, JPEG or PNG file of no more than 20 MB

Read [Documents you need](/services/applying-to-bir/documents) for the checklist and how to certify copies.
