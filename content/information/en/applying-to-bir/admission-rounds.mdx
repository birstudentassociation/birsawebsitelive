BIR is the Bachelor of Political Science in Politics and International Relations (International Program) at the Faculty of Political Science, Thammasat University. For 2027 entry there are five rounds, and you apply in one of them.

<Notice variant="info" title="The BIR website is the official source">

The BIR office publishes the official admission information at [birpolsci.com](https://www.birpolsci.com/prospectivestudents). Dates and requirements can change, so check that page before you apply.

</Notice>

## Which round suits you

If you are Thai, you can apply through

- [Inter-Portfolio 1](/services/applying-to-bir/inter-portfolio-1), if you are in Grade 12 (Mathayom 6) or have finished it, and you have an SAT result
- [Inter-Portfolio 2](/services/applying-to-bir/inter-portfolio-2), if you are in Mathayom 6, have a GPAX of at least 3.75 and have a portfolio
- [TCAS 3 and the BIR written exam](/services/applying-to-bir/tcas-3-written-exam), if you have a GPAX of at least 2.80 and want to sit the written exam

If you are not Thai, you apply through one of the two rounds in [Apply if you are not Thai](/services/applying-to-bir/non-thai-applicants). TCAS 3 is for Thai applicants only.

## Rules for every round

- You must not have confirmed your right to a place in the TCAS Clearing House system before you apply.
- You have one right to a place. Once you confirm it in a round, you cannot apply in a later round, unless you give up the right through the TCAS system within the set period. You can give it up and use it again only once.
- The application fee is not refundable.
- You certify that you meet the qualifications. The BIR office can refuse an unqualified applicant on the interview day.
- The BIR committee makes the final decision at every interview.
- Successful applicants confirm their place on [myTCAS](https://www.mytcas.com) in the period set for their round.

## How each round chooses students

| Round                   | How you are chosen                                                                                            |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inter-Portfolio 1       | The highest SAT Evidence-Based Reading and Writing scores are called to interview.                            |
| Inter-Portfolio 2       | The committee checks your portfolio against four kinds of achievement, then interviews you.                   |
| TCAS 3                  | The BIR written exam decides, then an interview. The exam is 40 percent multiple choice and 60 percent essay. |
| Non-Thai rounds 1 and 2 | The highest SAT Evidence-Based Reading and Writing scores are called to an online interview in English.       |
