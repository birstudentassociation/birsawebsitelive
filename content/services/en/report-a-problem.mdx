BIRSA exists to represent BIR students — if something isn't working, we'd rather know about it than have you deal with it quietly.

## What BIRSA can take up on your behalf

- **Recurring or structural issues** — something affecting more than just you, like a consistently locked classroom, a scheduling clash affecting a whole year group, or unclear communication from a department.
- **Facilities and environment** — problems with shared student spaces, equipment, or campus facilities relevant to BIR students.
- **Feedback that's hard to raise alone** — if you're not comfortable raising something directly, BIRSA can often raise it on a no-names basis with the relevant office.
- **Connecting you to the right office** — even when BIRSA can't fix something directly, we can usually tell you who can.

## What BIRSA can't do

We're a student association, not the university administration — we can't change grades, override academic decisions, or act as a substitute for the registrar or BIR office on official matters. For those, we'll point you to the right contact rather than take it on ourselves.

## How to report something

Use the [contact form](/services/contact?category=problem) and choose "A problem to report" as the category. Tell us:

1. What happened, as specifically as you can (dates, locations, what you tried already).
2. Whether this seems to affect just you or other students too.
3. Whether you're comfortable being named when we raise it, or would prefer we keep it anonymous.

We'll read every message and let you know what we can do — even if the honest answer is pointing you elsewhere.
