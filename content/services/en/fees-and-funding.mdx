A plain-language breakdown of what BIR costs per year, what smaller fees show up each semester, and where scholarship information tends to live.

## Tuition

- **Thai students**: 125,000 THB per year.
- **Non-Thai students**: 144,400 THB per year.

These figures cover the standard bi-semester year (August–December and January–May); an optional summer term is billed separately if you take one.

## Per-semester fees

On top of tuition, expect these smaller charges each semester:

| Fee | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Health fee | 125 THB |
| Sport fee | 200 THB |
| Activities fee | 200 THB |
| Library fee | 2,000 THB per year |

These are billed alongside tuition, not separately, so they should already appear on your registration invoice — but it's worth checking the total against this list if a bill looks off.

## Scholarships

Thammasat and the Faculty of Political Science offer a number of scholarships, need-based and merit-based. Some names you may come across:

- **Surin Pitsuwan Scholarship** — named for the late Thai diplomat and former ASEAN Secretary-General; typically aimed at students with strong academic standing and an interest in international affairs. Check with the BIR office for current eligibility and how to apply.
- **Faculty and university-wide scholarships** — the Faculty of Political Science and Thammasat's central student affairs office both run scholarship rounds; announcements usually go out at the start of each semester.

## Where to ask

For anything about your actual bill, payment deadlines, or scholarship applications, contact the BIR office directly — bir@tu.ac.th, 02-221-6111 ext. 3409 — or check the registrar at [reg.tu.ac.th](https://www.reg.tu.ac.th) for university-wide fee and finance announcements.
