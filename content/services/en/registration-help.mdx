Course registration happens at the start of every semester through the university registrar's system. This guide covers the basics — for anything programme-specific, the BIR office is your first stop.

## Before registration opens

Make sure your TU account is active (see the accounts guide) and that you know your registration group/time slot, which the registrar publishes ahead of each semester. Have a backup list of courses ready in case your first choices fill up.

## Registering for courses

1. Log in to [reg.tu.ac.th](https://www.reg.tu.ac.th) with your student ID and password.
2. Check the registration period for your year group — registration usually opens in slots, not all at once.
3. Select your courses and confirm. Keep a screenshot or printout of your confirmed schedule.
4. Check for any hold on your account (unpaid fees, missing documents) that could block registration, and clear it early.

## Add/drop basics

Most semesters have a short add/drop period right after registration closes, where you can still swap courses without penalty. After that window closes, dropping a course may affect your record differently (a "W" grade, for example), so check the exact deadlines published by the registrar for the current semester rather than relying on last year's dates.

## Where to get help

- **Academic advising / programme-specific questions**: the BIR office — bir@tu.ac.th, 02-221-6111 ext. 3409, or drop by in person at the Faculty of Political Science.
- **Registration system issues, deadlines, official forms**: the university registrar — [reg.tu.ac.th](https://www.reg.tu.ac.th).
- **General confusion about where to go**: ask BIRSA. We can't process registration for you, but we can point you to the right office.
