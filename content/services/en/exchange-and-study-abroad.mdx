BIR students have several routes to spend time abroad during the programme, from a semester exchange to a full double degree. This guide gives you the lay of the land — the BIR office holds the exact application windows and paperwork.

## Two exchange routes

Thammasat runs exchange opportunities at two levels:

1. **University-wide exchange** — open to students across Thammasat, run through the university's international affairs office, with partner universities across many countries.
2. **Faculty-only exchange** — smaller, more competitive routes arranged directly by the Faculty of Political Science, often with partner institutions that have an existing relationship with BIR.

Ask the BIR office which route fits your interests and timeline — the application process and deadlines differ between the two.

## Double-degree partners

BIR has double-degree arrangements with:

- **Meiji University**, Japan
- **Aberystwyth University**, UK
- **University of Bristol**, UK

A double degree is a longer commitment than a single-semester exchange — you'll typically spend an extended period at the partner university and graduate with degrees from both institutions. Talk to the BIR office early (ideally in your second year) if this interests you, since planning ahead matters for course sequencing.

## Closer to home: the BIR field trip and BISC

Not every international experience means leaving the country for a semester:

- **Third-year field trip** — BIR runs an annual field trip for third-year students, typically involving travel and a study component tied to the programme's international relations focus.
- **Bangkok International Student Conference (BISC)** — a conference hosted in Bangkok that brings together students for discussion and networking on international affairs; a lower-commitment way to get international exposure without leaving Thailand.

## Internships

BIR also supports internship opportunities, both domestic and international, which can be a good complement to (or alternative for) formal exchange if your timeline or budget doesn't fit a semester abroad.

## Where to start

Speak to the BIR office (bir@tu.ac.th, 02-221-6111 ext. 3409) about which of these fits your year group, GPA requirements, and budget — exchange and double-degree places are competitive and have fixed application windows each year.
