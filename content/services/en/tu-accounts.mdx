Almost everything at Thammasat runs through one login: your **TU student ID**. It's worth getting this set up properly in your first week, because it unlocks wifi, the library, course registration and more.

## Your username and password

Your TU account username is your **student ID number**. Your initial password is your **citizen ID number** (or passport number, if you're an international student without a Thai citizen ID).

Change this password as soon as you can — a lot of students forget and get locked out later when they can't remember what they originally used.

## Check your account is active

Before you rely on it for registration or wifi, check that your account is active:

1. Go to [restapi.tu.ac.th](https://restapi.tu.ac.th) and use the account-check tool.
2. Enter your student ID.
3. If it shows as active, you're good to go. If not, contact the registrar (see below) rather than IT — activation issues are usually a registration-office matter.

## What this account unlocks

- **Wifi** — connect to the TU wifi network using the same student ID and password.
- **Library account** — Thammasat University Library uses the same login for borrowing, e-resources, and study room bookings.
- **Course registration** — you'll need this account to register for courses and check your results at [reg.tu.ac.th](https://www.reg.tu.ac.th).

## If something isn't working

If your account won't activate or your password reset isn't going through, the registrar's office is the right place to start — see [reg.tu.ac.th](https://www.reg.tu.ac.th) for contact channels, or ask the BIR office (<Email address="bir@tu.ac.th" />, 02-221-6111 ext. 3409) to point you in the right direction.
