There are several ways to get in touch with BIRSA, depending on what's easiest for you.

## Contact form

The quickest way to reach the committee directly is our [contact form](/services/contact). Messages sent through the form go straight to the committee by email.

## Email and socials

- **Email:** bir@tu.ac.th
- **Instagram:** [@student_birsa](https://www.instagram.com/student_birsa/)
- **Facebook:** [BIR Student Association](https://www.facebook.com/BIRStudentAssociation/)

Social media is generally the fastest way to catch event announcements and quick updates, while email and the contact form are better for anything that needs a considered reply.

## Office address and phone

BIRSA operates alongside the BIR programme at:

BIR, Faculty of Political Science, Thammasat University, 2 Prachan Road, Bangkok 10200

Phone: 02-221-6111 ext. 3409

Note that this is the BIR programme office's contact information — for official academic matters, this is also the correct first point of contact. BIRSA itself is student-run and unofficial; see [What is BIRSA?](/about/birsa) for how the two relate.
