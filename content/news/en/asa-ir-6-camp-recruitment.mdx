Applications for **ASA IR 6**, this year's volunteer development camp, closed at 23:59 on 26
September 2026. ASA IR had extended the deadline by 2 days and said it would not extend again.

The camp renovates a local school during the December break.

<Notice variant="warning" title="Applications closed at 23:59 on 26 September 2026">
  The application form is no longer open. Follow [@asa.ir.tu](https://www.instagram.com/asa.ir.tu/)
  on Instagram for updates from ASA IR.
</Notice>

## Who could apply

Applications were open to any Thammasat University student, from any faculty, campus or year.
