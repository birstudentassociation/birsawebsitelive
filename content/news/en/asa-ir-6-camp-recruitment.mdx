Spend part of the December break renovating a local school. **ASA IR 6** is this year's volunteer
development camp, and applications close soon.

<Notice variant="warning" title="Applications close at 23:59 on 24 September 2026">
  [Apply for ASA IR 6](https://qrfy.io/ELpaq0wm4D). This is the link the QR code on the poster
  opens. You can also use the link in the [@asa.ir.tu](https://www.instagram.com/asa.ir.tu/)
  Instagram bio.
</Notice>

## Who can apply

Any Thammasat University student can apply, from any faculty, campus or year.

## Where the camp is

| Detail | Information                                                                                                    |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Place  | Thai Hill Tribes Community Education Center, Ban Thi Pha Lae, Mae Long Subdistrict, Omkoi District, Chiang Mai |
| Dates  | 13 to 24 December 2026                                                                                         |
