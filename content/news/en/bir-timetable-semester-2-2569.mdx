The BIR office has published the class and exam timetable for semester 2 of AY 2569 (2026/2027).
Classes run from 4 January to 1 May 2027. Midterm exams fall on 23 to 27 February 2027, and final
exams run from 3 to 18 May 2027. Every date below is in 2027 unless the text says otherwise.

## Tuition and fees

Registration for semester 2 runs from 30 November to 4 December 2026 at
[reg.tu.ac.th](https://www.reg.tu.ac.th). Registration opens at 09:00 on the first day and closes at
22:30 on the last day.

You pay through the TU GREATS app from 30 November 2026 to 1 January 2027. Payment opens at 09:00 on
the first day and closes at 22:30 on the last day.

The BIR office sets the total cost by the number of credits you register.

## Rooms and times can change

The BIR office can change rooms and times. Check [birpolsci.com](https://www.birpolsci.com) and the
BIR Program Facebook page for updates. The [academic calendar for AY 2569](/en/news/academic-calendar-2569)
lists the Registrar's dates for the whole university.
