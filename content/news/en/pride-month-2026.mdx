BIRSA joins in celebrating, and advocating for, the rights and freedoms of Pride Month 2026. Pride is not only a celebration; it's a long journey rooted in struggle, carried from the past into the present.

Real progress has been made, including the recent passage of Thailand's marriage-equality bill. The work is not finished: more than 60 countries still criminalise love, and young people continue to face pressure for being who they are.

Equality has to be built into policy and structure, not just shown through rainbow campaigns for a month each year. Visibility matters most when it's backed by fair laws and a genuinely inclusive society, and that's the Pride BIRSA stands for.
