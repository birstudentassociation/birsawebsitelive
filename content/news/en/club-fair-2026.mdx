Every club at BIR sets up a stall in one place, one afternoon, so you can see what's on offer before committing to anything. Whether you're into debate, media, sports, or something more niche, this is the easiest way to find your people.

Can't decide? Walk the whole fair first, then circle back. There's no rule that says you can only join one.
