The free shuttle at Tha Prachan now runs with 2 buses instead of 4. Since 1 October 2026 each line has had only 1 bus, so you wait longer and every bus is fuller.

The Thammasat University Student Union (TUSU) and the Thammasat University Student Council (TUSC) are asking the University to buy 2 more buses in time for April 2027. They need as many signatures as possible to show the University's executives how many students this affects. BIR Student Association (BIRSA) asks every BIR student to sign.

**[Sign the shuttle bus petition](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdWycAgSYofp569-oTp_qzMve9c9kATMW71LAFRhYkDiqBYMg/viewform)**

## Why your name matters

TUSU and TUSC have raised the cut from 4 buses to 2 with the University since 2025. They have asked again and again what students should do during the changeover. They still have not had a clear answer from the executives.

A petition changes that. A complaint from a few students is easy to set aside. A list with thousands of names shows the executives that the shuttle is something the whole campus depends on. The more students sign, the harder the request is to turn down.

You do not need to use the shuttle every day to sign. If you have ever missed a class, a train or a meal waiting for it, your name counts.

## What changed

The University used to lease the shuttle buses. The leases caused problems with drivers, service quality and the lease terms, and the University paid rent on every bus. It decided to buy its own buses instead, so the shuttle is now University property. It bought 2.

| Line            | Until 30 September 2026 | From 1 October 2026 |
| --------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------- |
| Sanam Chai Line | 2 leased buses          | 1 University bus    |
| Pinklao Line    | 2 leased buses          | 1 University bus    |

## How it happened

| When | What happened                                                                                                            |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 2025 | The University announces its plan to buy buses.                                                                          |
| 2025 | TUSU and TUSC raise concerns about the number of buses, and ask about backup buses and plans for the changeover.         |
| 2025 | The University goes ahead with the purchase.                                                                             |
| 2026 | The leases end and the new buses start running. 2 buses are left.                                                        |
| 2026 | TUSU and TUSC meet the University's executives to find stopgap measures, and propose that the University buy more buses. |

## What TUSU and TUSC have done so far

- **Sanam Chai Line.** They have talked to the bus service carrying people to pay respects to the royal remains about adding a stop in front of Thammasat on the Sanam Luang side.
- **Pinklao Line.** They have asked for extra buses that carry dormitory residents only.

Next, they will apply to the University's fee fund for 2 more buses, to be running by April 2027. The petition goes with that application.

## Until more buses arrive

Check the [shuttle timetable](/student-life/getting-around/shuttle-bus) before you leave, because each line now has fewer departures. If a shuttle time does not suit you, use the M2 bus from Sanam Luang, or public bus routes 53, 43 or 15.
