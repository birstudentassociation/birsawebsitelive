Congratulations, and a very warm welcome to all incoming BIR18 students — you're officially part of BIR and the Faculty of Political Science, Thammasat University.

BIRSA is here to help make the transition as smooth as possible. Check the original Instagram post for a QR code linking to everything you'll need to get started: official communication channels, the BIR18 LINE group, a guide to new-student registration, and recommendations for accommodation and places to eat near campus.

Take some time to go through it all carefully, and stay tuned for orientation and other faculty activities coming up soon.
