The Marine Department announced today that a stretch of the Chao Phraya becomes a temporary navigation control area on three afternoons this month, while the Royal Thai Navy rehearses the royal barge procession for the celebration of Her Majesty the Queen's 48th birthday anniversary on 3 June 2026.

For the Chao Phraya Express Boat, this is not a delay. The middle of the line simply stops running, and the piers for Tha Prachan are in the middle.

<Notice variant="warning" title="17, 20 and 27 August 2026, from 12:30">
  No express boat service between **Payap** and **Ratchawong**. **Tha Chang** and **Tha Phra Nok**,
  the two stops this site's route advice uses for campus, are both inside that stretch. The notice
  says service resumes "until the rehearsal is finished" and gives no reopening time.
</Notice>

## The three afternoons

| Date           | Day      | Express boat affected               |
| -------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------- |
| 17 August 2026 | Monday   | From 12:30 until the rehearsal ends |
| 20 August 2026 | Thursday | From 12:30 until the rehearsal ends |
| 27 August 2026 | Thursday | From 12:30 until the rehearsal ends |

Mornings are normal. An earlier restriction starting at 09:00 applies only to seagoing ships, tugs
towing cargo barges, and vessels carrying dangerous goods, so express boats run their usual service
up to 12:30. Everything below applies from 12:30 onwards.

## What the boat does from 12:30

The control area runs between **Krung Thon (Sang Hi) Bridge** and **Phra Pokklao Bridge**. The
express boat is cut into two halves that no longer meet:

| Where you are                | What runs from 12:30                                            |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| North of the closure         | Boats pick up and drop off at **Payap pier and upriver**        |
| Between Payap and Ratchawong | **Nothing.** No express boat calls at any pier here             |
| South of the closure         | Boats pick up and drop off at **Ratchawong pier and downriver** |

There is no shuttle across the gap. If your pier is inside it, the express boat is not an option
that afternoon, in either direction.

## The piers you lose

Every express boat stop between the two bridges is inside the closed stretch. Two of them matter for
getting to Tha Prachan:

- **Tha Chang** — the stop you walk from to campus, and the end of the BTS Silom Line route (Taksin,
  then the express boat north).
- **Tha Phra Nok** — the stop you use before the cross-river ferry to Tha Wang Lang.

<Notice title="Coming from Taksin or Sathorn?">
  The southbound half of the line still runs, but it now ends at **Ratchawong**, well short of
  campus. From there you are walking or taking a bus through the old city, so the land routes below
  are usually faster from the start.
</Notice>

## Getting to campus on those afternoons

- **Cross before 12:30.** The simplest plan. Take your usual boat in the morning and stay on the
  campus side.
- **MRT Blue Line to Sanam Chai**, then bus 32 or 53, or walk through Rattanakosin Island.
- **Buses to Sanam Luang or Tha Phra Chan**: 1, 3, 15, 19, 25, 30, 32, 53, 64, 80, 91, 123, 124,
  189, 203, 208, plus air-conditioned ปอ.32 and ปอ.524.

Full details of every route are on [Getting around](/en/student-life/home/getting-around).

<Accordion summary="What the Marine Department notice actually says about the express boat">
  The notice designates the Chao Phraya between Krung Thon (Sang Hi) Bridge and Phra Pokklao Bridge
  a temporary navigation control area under section 52 bis of the Navigation in Thai Waters Act,
  B.E. 2456, as amended.

From 09:00 the Marine Department asks seagoing vessels, tugs towing cargo barges and vessels
carrying dangerous goods to avoid the area. From 12:30 the request extends to all vessels, and the
express boat is named specifically: northern services may pick up and drop off passengers from
Payap pier upwards, and southern services from Ratchawong pier downwards, in both cases from 12:30
"until the rehearsal is finished".

Operators who ignore the notice face a fine of 500 to 5,000 baht and suspension of the boat
master's certificate for up to six months, so expect the cut-off to be observed strictly rather
than loosely.

</Accordion>

<Notice>
  Rehearsal timings can shift. Check the operator's announcements on the day before relying on a
  boat, and allow extra time for the buses, which absorb the diverted passengers.
</Notice>
