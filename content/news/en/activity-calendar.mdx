BIRSA updates this page at the start of each month with the key dates for BIR students. The
latest month comes first.

BIRSA announces BIR's registration, payment and add-drop dates separately.

Dates can still change, so follow BIRSA's channels for updates.

## October 2026

October carries memories that shaped Thammasat and the democratic history of Thailand. BIRSA marks
them alongside the ASA IR 6 fundraising, the BIR Seminar and TPC Open House.

Classes are online on 12 October and from 14 to 16 October, and 13 October is a public holiday. Read
the [advisory on online classes](/en/advisory) for what changes.

| Date             | What is on                                                                                    |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 6 October        | 50 years since 6 October 1976. The 6 October Memorial at Tha Prachan remembers those who died |
| 7 to 29 October  | ASA IR 6 fundraising                                                                          |
| 12 October       | Classes online. The BIR Office is closed                                                      |
| 13 October       | Public holiday, no classes                                                                    |
| 14 to 16 October | Classes online. The BIR Office is closed                                                      |
| 14 October       | 14 October 1973 uprising anniversary (วันมหาวิปโยค)                                           |
| 22 October       | BIR Seminar                                                                                   |
| 25 October       | 22 years since the Tak Bai incident                                                           |
| 31 October       | TPC Open House 2026                                                                           |
