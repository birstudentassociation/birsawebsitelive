BIRSA updates this page at the start of each month with the key dates for BIR students. The
latest month comes first.

BIRSA announces BIR's registration, payment and add-drop dates separately.

Dates can still change, so follow BIRSA's channels for updates.

## August 2026

Welcome back, BIR. August opens the new semester with two orientations and the Tha Prachan welcome
activities.

| Date            | What is on                                                    |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 August        | Faculty of Political Science orientation                      |
| 2 August        | TU Freshy Orientation                                         |
| 11 to 12 August | Back to School by TPC Music                                   |
| 11 to 19 August | [TPC Crazy Week](/en/news/tpc-crazy-week-2026)                |
| 12 August       | Mother's Day, a public holiday with no classes                |
| 16 August       | Thai Peace Day (วันสันติภาพไทย)                               |
| 19 August       | TPC Firstmeet, from 16:00 at the Main Auditorium, Tha Prachan |
