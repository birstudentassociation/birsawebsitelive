BIRSA is pleased to officially announce the appointment of the BIR Student Association committee — the student committee of the Faculty of Political Science's Politics and International Relations (International Program) — for academic year 2026.

The newly appointed committee will be working on behalf of all BIR students throughout the year ahead. Follow BIRSA's channels for updates on what's coming next.
