BIRSA is recruiting passionate sub-committee members for the 2026–2027 academic year. This round is open exclusively to students with ID numbers 66 through 68.

Key dates:
- Applications open: June 15–25, 2026
- Interviews (hybrid, onsite/online): June 27–28, 2026
- Results announced: by June 30, 2026

Ten sub-committees are recruiting this round:
- Student Activities
- Merchandise Officer
- Foreign Students Assistance Officer
- Secretariat
- Academic Affairs
- Treasurer and Sponsor
- General Coordinator
- Public Relations (Graphic Design, Video Editor, Content Creator)
- Rights Advocate and Student Welfare Affairs
- Sport Coordinator

Apply by scanning the QR code in the original Instagram post. If any of these roles sound like you, now's the time.
