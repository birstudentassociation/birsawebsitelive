BIRSA is recruiting sub-committee members for the 2026 to 2027 academic year. This round is open only to students with ID numbers 66 to 68.

These are the key dates for round 1.

| Stage                                 | Date               |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Applications open                     | 15 to 25 June 2026 |
| Interviews (hybrid, onsite or online) | 27 to 28 June 2026 |
| Results announced                     | By 30 June 2026    |

BIRSA is recruiting for 10 sub-committees this round.

- Student Activities
- Merchandise Officer
- Foreign Students Assistance Officer
- Secretariat
- Academic Affairs
- Treasurer and Sponsor
- General Coordinator
- Public Relations (Graphic Design, Video Editor, Content Creator)
- Rights Advocate and Student Welfare Affairs
- Sport Coordinator

Apply by scanning the QR code in the original Instagram post.
