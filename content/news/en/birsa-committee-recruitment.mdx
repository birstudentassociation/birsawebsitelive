BIRSA is run entirely by BIR students, for BIR students — and every year we open up committee positions to anyone who wants to shape what that looks like. No prior experience is required, just a willingness to show up and follow through.

Roles typically cover areas like events, communications, student welfare, and finance. Each comes with a clear scope, so you're not signing up for a vague, open-ended commitment.

If you've ever thought "someone should really fix this" about student life at BIR, committee is how you become that someone.

Applications open this month — watch this page and our Instagram for the exact form and deadline.
