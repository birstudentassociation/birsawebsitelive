The BIR office has published the internship schedule for students with ID 67. The internship lasts 8 weeks and is the course PI574 in the summer session of academic year 2569 (2026/2027).

| What                                                                | When                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Internship Request Form, if you are still applying                  | By the end of March 2027       |
| Secured Internship Form, required for everyone                      | By the end of April 2027       |
| Internship orientation, where you collect your Letter of Internship | May 2027                       |
| Internship, 8 weeks                                                 | Usually 7 June to 30 July 2027 |

You must also attend at least 2 training sessions, register for PI574 and pay the fee. The fee is 19,735 baht for Thai students and 24,510 baht for international students.

The internship is optional. Students who complete it receive 3 credits, which count as 1 free elective course.

[Read the internship guide](/student-life/studying/internship) for the steps for each letter, the rules for applying to more than 1 organisation and who to contact.
