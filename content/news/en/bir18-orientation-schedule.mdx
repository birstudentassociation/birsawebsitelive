Welcome, BIR18. The programme schedule for orientation day is below.

<Notice>Arrive by 8:00 for event registration. The day runs until 16:00, so plan to stay for the full program.</Notice>

## Program schedule

| Time | Session |
| --- | --- |
| 8:00 to 8:20 | Event registration |
| 8:20 to 8:30 | Welcome address for new students (by the MC) |
| 8:30 to 8:40 | Opening remarks by Assoc. Prof. Puli Fuwongcharoen, Dean of the Faculty of Political Science |
| 8:40 to 9:15 | Introduction to the BIR program, faculty members, and academics by Assoc. Prof. Dr. Jittipat Poonkham, Associate Dean for Academic and International Affairs |
| 9:15 to 9:45 | Course registration guidance (by BIR staff) |
| 9:45 to 10:00 | Refreshment break |
| 10:00 to 12:00 | Introduction to TU Library services (by library staff) |
| 12:00 to 13:00 | Lunch break |
| 13:00 to 16:00 | Activity introduction and club presentation by BIRSA |

The afternoon session with BIRSA (13:00 to 16:00) includes:

- BIRSA presentation, "What is it like to be a BIR student"
- Club presentations
- Club showcasing

## Highlights of the day

- Meet the Dean and faculty members.
- Get essential guidance on course registration and library services.
- An afternoon showcase by BIRSA covering what it's like to be a BIR student, with club presentations.
