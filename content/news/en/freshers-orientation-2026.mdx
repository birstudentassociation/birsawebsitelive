Orientation is your first proper day as a BIR student — a chance to meet the people you'll be spending the next four years with, put faces to names, and get a straight-talking introduction to how things work here.

Expect short talks from the programme and from BIRSA, a campus walk-through so Tha Prachan stops feeling like a maze, and time built in just to talk to seniors who've been exactly where you are.

Wear something comfortable — you'll be on your feet for parts of the day — and bring a bottle of water; Bangkok in August is hot.

We'll share the full run-of-day and a map closer to the date. If you can't make it, let BIRSA know and we'll make sure you don't miss anything important.
