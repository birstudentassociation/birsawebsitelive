The BIR Student Association (BIRSA) has published the official candidate list for the BIR18 Student
Council election 2026. Six students are standing, all under the BIR18 Rising Star party.

<Notice title="Policy presentation, 19 August 2026">
  The candidates set out their policies in Room R.102 from 12:30 to 13:00. Come and hear them before
  you vote on 24 August.
</Notice>

## Who is standing

BIRSA published the names in Thai, so they appear here as announced.

| Candidate               | Standing for            |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| จีรณา แสงสว่าง          | Class President         |
| วิชญ์ เอี่ยมวุฒิกร      | Rangsit Coordinator     |
| พิมพ์มาดา ตังอนุสรณ์สุข | Tha Prachan Coordinator |
| วรัญญา มณีธรรม          | Public Relations        |
| ตะวันฉาย อุตมงค์        | Activity Coordinator    |
| วิศวกร วงศ์พิภพมงคล     | Academic Affairs        |

## The policy presentation

| Detail | Information                                                                        |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Date   | Wednesday 19 August 2026                                                           |
| Time   | 12:30 to 13:00                                                                     |
| Venue  | Room R.102, Faculty of Political Science, Thammasat University, Tha Prachan campus |

The candidates explain their vision for the cohort and the policies they want to run on. Students
can listen, ask questions, and put their own views to the candidates.
