Why do we feel we have to be productive all the time? We compete, work on ourselves and chase other
people's idea of success, until the line between work and rest fades. Then we burn out and blame
ourselves for not trying hard enough.

**24/7 No Rest Under Capitalism** asks whether that pressure is something we want, or something
capitalism has locked us into. The BIR Student Association (BIRSA) runs the seminar as part of the
BIRSA Seminar Series.

## What the seminar covers

The speakers talk through

- capitalism and how it shapes the way we work
- productivity culture
- burnout, and why it is a structural problem as well as a personal one

The seminar ends with an open question and answer session.

## The speakers

**Assoc. Prof. Dr Sorawit Chaiyanam** lectures in the Department of International Relations,
Faculty of Political Science, Chulalongkorn University.

**Asst. Prof. Dr Attasit Sittidumrong** lectures in Politics and Government, Faculty of Political
Science, Thammasat University.

**Omar Nunanan** is a former spokesperson for the election monitoring network We Watch. He is now
an activist and writer, with academic and opinion pieces on politics, society and burnout among
people who work in political movements.
