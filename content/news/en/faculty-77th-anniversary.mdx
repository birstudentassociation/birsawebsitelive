Today marks the 77th anniversary of the Faculty of Political Science, Thammasat University. The faculty was founded in 1949 (พ.ศ. 2492) under the leadership of Professor Direk Jayanama, who also served as its first dean.

The world has changed a great deal since then, but the faculty's core mission has not: to produce graduates who serve society and the people. Over the decades it has continually adapted to meet the times, and today's students stand on the shoulders of tens of thousands of alumni who came before them.

Happy 77th anniversary to the Faculty of Political Science.
