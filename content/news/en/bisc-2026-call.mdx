The Bangkok International Student Conference (BISC) brings together students from across the region to debate and workshop real political and international-relations questions. BIR students take part most years, and it's a strong line for anyone interested in diplomacy, policy work, or graduate study.

This year BIRSA is coordinating applications on behalf of interested BIR students. You don't need prior conference experience — you do need to be willing to read in, prepare a short position, and show up ready to engage.

Places are limited, so apply early. BIRSA will shortlist and confirm delegates directly by email.

Questions about the process go to BIRSA, not the conference organisers directly — we'll pass on anything we can't answer ourselves.
