Registration for semester 1 2026 (August–December) is now open through the TU registrar system. It's worth doing this early rather than at the deadline, when the system tends to slow down under demand.

Before you submit, double-check your course list against your programme requirements, confirm you don't have a scheduling clash between sections, and make sure any holds on your account (library, tuition, documents) are cleared first.

If a course you need is full, don't panic — reach out to the programme office about waitlists or alternative sections before assuming you're stuck.

If registration itself is giving you trouble — login issues, payment errors, anything technical — BIRSA can point you to the right office to sort it out.
