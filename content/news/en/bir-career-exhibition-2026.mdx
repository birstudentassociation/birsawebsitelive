The BIR Student Association (BIRSA) holds the **BIR Career Exhibition 2026** on **Wednesday
11 November 2026**. It is for students looking for an internship, and students from any faculty can
come.

| When  | Details                                                    |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Date  | Wednesday 11 November 2026                                 |
| Time  | From 14:00 to 17:30                                        |
| Place | Room 103, Faculty of Political Science, Tha Prachan campus |

## Who will have a booth

Organisations from these sectors will have booths and tell you about opportunities to work with
them.

- the private sector
- government agencies
- non-governmental organisations (NGOs) and intergovernmental organisations (IGOs)
- think tanks and research institutes
- foundations

BIRSA will announce the line-up of organisations before the event.

using the online form.
