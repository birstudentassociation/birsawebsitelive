The BIR Student Association (BIRSA) holds the **BIR Career Exhibition 2026** on **Wednesday
11 November 2026**. 12 organisations from NGOs, IGOs, the private sector, government, foundations,
think tanks and research will have booths. Students from any faculty can come and ask them about
internships and jobs.
