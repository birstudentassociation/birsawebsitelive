**ASA IR 6**, this year's volunteer development camp, is taking donations. The money goes towards
renovating the school building for the children at the Thai Hill Tribes Community Education
Center, Ban Thi Pha Lae, Omkoi District, Chiang Mai.

The camp runs from 13 to 24 December 2026.
