Semester 2 of academic year 2569 (2026/2027) runs from 4 January to 1 May 2027. BIR registration opens on 30 November 2026, so you have a few weeks to decide what to take.

## Dates

| Date                          | What happens                                                            |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 30 November to 4 December     | BIR course registration, opening at 09:00 and closing at 22:30 each day |
| 30 November to 1 January 2027 | Pay your fees through the TU GREATS app                                 |
| 4 January 2027                | Classes begin                                                           |
| 4 to 17 January               | Add-drop                                                                |
| 23 to 27 February             | BIR midterm exams                                                       |
| Until 14 March                | Withdraw from a course with a W                                         |
| 3 to 18 May                   | Final exams                                                             |

The BIR office sets BIR's registration, payment and exam dates. These can differ from the Registrar's regular-programme dates. Read the [BIR timetable for semester 2](/en/news/bir-timetable-semester-2-2569) for your year's classes, exams and fees. Read the [academic calendar for AY 2569](/en/news/academic-calendar-2569) for the full university dates.

## What to do before registration

1. Check your year's class timetable in the [BIR timetable for semester 2](/en/news/bir-timetable-semester-2-2569).
2. Check the prerequisites for each course you want. For example, PI370 needs PI280, and PI320 needs PI211.
3. Pick your electives. Year 2 students pick 1 elective course, and Year 3 students pick 5.
4. Read the reviews of the electives you are considering.
5. Check the fee table in the timetable post and plan how you will pay by 1 January 2027.

## Use the study plan

[Plan your BIR degree](/services/study-plan) checks your prerequisites and shows which courses the faculty teaches in semester 2. It does not let you add a course taught only in semester 2 to a semester 1 term. It also shows the BIR registration and add-drop dates for the terms in your plan, and you can download them to add to your own calendar.

Your plan stays in your browser, and BIRSA does not receive it. The service takes about 10 minutes. Have the first two digits of your student ID ready.

## Read course reviews

The [course catalogue](/student-life/course-reviews) has a page for every BIR course, with its credits, prerequisites and what it counts towards. Students can also send a review of a course they have taken. Open a course page, or compare two courses side by side, before you choose your electives.

## Keep the dates in your calendar

The activity calendar on the [BIRSA home page](/) lists BIRSA activities and university dates. You can subscribe to it in Apple Calendar, Outlook or Google Calendar, and your app picks up new dates on its own.

## How BIRSA can help

BIRSA is a student association, not a university office. The BIR office answers academic questions and announces BIR's registration and add-drop dates. Email bir@tu.ac.th or call 02-221-6111 ext. 3409.

If you are not sure who to ask, use [Find where to take a question or problem](/contact/where-to-go). Send BIRSA your question and BIRSA will answer it or tell you which office can.
