Welcome back, BIR. August is packed with events to kick off the new semester: two orientations, the first week of class, add-drop, and the Tha Prachan campus welcome activities.

- August 1: Faculty of Political Science orientation
- August 2: TU Freshy Orientation
- August 3: Class begins
- August 3 to 4: BIR18 student ID card pick-up
- August 4 to 16: Add-drop period and late registration
- August 11 to 12: Back to School by TPC Music
- August 11 to 19: [TPC Crazy Week](/en/news/tpc-crazy-week-2026)
- August 12: Mother's Day, a public holiday with no classes
- August 16: Thai Peace Day (วันสันติภาพไทย)
- August 19: TPC Firstmeet, from 16:00 at the Main Auditorium, Tha Prachan

Dates are subject to change. Follow BIRSA's channels for updates.
