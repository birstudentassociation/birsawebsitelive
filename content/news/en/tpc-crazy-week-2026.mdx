The semester has barely started and Tha Prachan is already in costume. **TPC Crazy Week** runs from **11 to 19 August 2026** as part of TPC Firstmeet 2026, and the rule is in the name: go crazy, or go home.

<Notice variant="success" title="🎈 Go Crazy or Go Home — 11 to 19 August 2026">
  Six themed days. Dress up, take a photo, and you could be the Best Outfit of the Day. No sign-up,
  no fee, no excuse: just wear the theme and show up on campus.
</Notice>

## 📅 Day by day

<Accordion summary="🎒 11 August — Back to School">
  Time travel back to secondary school. White shirt, pleated skirt or khaki shorts, the tie, the
  house pin — the full kit. Finish the look with the accessories that came with it: a leather
  satchel or a wheeled backpack, sticky-note tabs, a correction tape, the scientific calculator you
  swore you would never need again, and black school shoes or white canvas sneakers.
</Accordion>

<Accordion summary="🎬 13 August — Blockbuster Day">
  Favourite film characters, ready to walk off the screen. Superheroes, villains, dolls, wizards,
  minions, inflatable dinosaurs — anything cinema. The more recognisable the silhouette from across
  the courtyard, the better.
</Accordion>

<Accordion summary="🧺 14 August — Anything But A Bag">
  One rule, and only one: **no bags**. Bring your books and your laptop in an umbrella, a picnic
  basket, a bucket, a shopping basket, a blanket roll, a plush toy, a toaster oven — whatever holds
  a pencil case and survives a day on campus. No bag? No worries.
</Accordion>

<Accordion summary="🌾 17 August — Thai Ban Style">
  Countryside Thai, done properly. Football shorts, an oversized checked shirt, a sun hat or a
  wide-brim farming hat, wraparound sunglasses, a pakama, flip-flops, a woven shoulder bag, a cooler
  bucket, a woven fan, a jar of balm, and a lottery ticket for luck.
</Accordion>

<Accordion summary="👕 18 August — Jersey Day">
  The one everyone owns. Wear your TU jersey, black or white, and start the journey with the rest of
  Tha Prachan.
</Accordion>

<Accordion summary="🔮 19 August — Dreamland Aura">
  Pick the colour that says the most about you and wear it head to toe. See the aura key below for
  what each colour stands for.
</Accordion>

## 🏆 Best Outfit of the Day

Every themed day has a winner, and joining takes about a minute:

1. **Dress for the day's theme.** 👕
2. **Take a photo and join the "Add Yours" sticker** on the [@tusu.tpc](https://www.instagram.com/tusu.tpc/) Instagram Story. 📸
3. **Wait for the announcement** — the day's winner goes up on the [@tusu.tpc](https://www.instagram.com/tusu.tpc/) Story at **19:00**. 🎁

<Notice variant="warning" title="🎟️ What you're playing for">
  Catch the judges' eye and you walk away with **free cinema tickets from Major Group**. Judging is
  per day, so a miss on Tuesday costs you nothing on Thursday.
</Notice>

## 💡 Getting the most out of the week

- **Borrow before you buy.** Half of these themes live in someone's wardrobe already — ask around the batch before ordering anything.
- **Team up.** Group looks read far better in a Story than a solo attempt, and Blockbuster Day is made for a cast.
- **Dress for a full teaching day.** Tha Prachan in August is hot and often wet; whatever you wear has to survive lectures, stairs, and the walk to lunch.
- **Post early.** Winners are picked by 19:00, so a photo added in the evening may be too late to be seen.
