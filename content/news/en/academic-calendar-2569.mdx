Thammasat University's Registrar has published the academic calendar for AY 2569 (2026/2027). The
university-wide dates that apply to BIR students are below, with what is on now and what is coming
up.

**BIR's semester 2 dates are now in the BIR section below.** BIR is a special programme, so some
of its dates differ from the regular-programme dates in the Registrar's calendar. The midterm exams
are one example. Pick BIR programme to see BIR's own semester 2 dates, and follow them where the two
differ. The BIR office announces BIR's semester 1 dates separately on
[birpolsci.com](https://www.birpolsci.com) and on the BIR Program Facebook page.

The calendar now includes every public holiday from August 2026 to July 2027.

You can filter the dates by type and add them straight to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or
Outlook.
