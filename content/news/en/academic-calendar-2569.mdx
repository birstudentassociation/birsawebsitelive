Thammasat University's Registrar has published the academic calendar for AY 2569 (2026/2027). The
university-wide dates that apply to BIR students are below.

**BIRSA announces BIR's course registration and tuition payment dates separately.** BIR is a special
programme, so its registration and payment windows do not match the regular-programme dates in the
Registrar's calendar. Do not use the regular-programme windows for your own registration or payment.

## Semester 1 (August to December 2026)

| Date                       | What happens                                                               |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 3 August                   | Semester 1 begins                                                          |
| 3 to 16 August             | Late registration and add-drop                                             |
| 3 to 16 August             | Apply to graduate                                                          |
| 3 to 17 August             | Pay for late registration and add-drop                                     |
| 18 August                  | Late-add penalty fees begin                                                |
| 19 August to 12 October    | Withdraw a course with a W online                                          |
| 1 September                | Last day to register or take leave before the university removes your name |
| 14 September               | Midterm seating plans go up                                                |
| 20 to 27 September         | Midterm exams                                                              |
| 13 October to 22 November  | Withdraw with a W through the faculty, with the dean's approval            |
| 16 November                | Final exam seating plans go up                                             |
| 21 November                | Last day of semester 1                                                     |
| 23 November to 11 December | Final exams                                                                |

The final exam period is not continuous. Exams run 23 to 30 November, then 1 to 4, 8 to 9 and
11 December 2026.

## Semester 2 (January to May 2027)

| Date                   | What happens                                                               |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 4 January              | Semester 2 begins                                                          |
| 4 to 17 January        | Late registration and add-drop                                             |
| 4 to 17 January        | Apply to graduate                                                          |
| 4 to 18 January        | Pay for late registration and add-drop                                     |
| 18 January to 14 March | Withdraw a course with a W online                                          |
| 19 January             | Late-add penalty fees begin                                                |
| 2 February             | Last day to register or take leave before the university removes your name |
| 15 February            | Midterm seating plans go up                                                |
| 21 to 28 February      | Midterm exams                                                              |
| 15 March to 2 May      | Withdraw with a W through the faculty, with the dean's approval            |
| 12 to 18 April         | No classes for Songkran                                                    |
| 19 April               | Classes resume                                                             |
| 26 April               | Final exam seating plans go up                                             |
| 1 May                  | Last day of semester 2                                                     |
| 3 to 18 May            | Final exams                                                                |

This exam period is not continuous either. Exams run on 3 May, then 5 to 18 May 2027.

## Summer session (June to July 2027)

| Date              | What happens                                                    |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 7 June            | Summer session begins                                           |
| 7 to 13 June      | Late registration and add-drop                                  |
| 7 to 13 June      | Apply to graduate                                               |
| 7 to 14 June      | Pay for late registration and add-drop                          |
| 15 June           | Late-add penalty fees begin                                     |
| 16 June to 4 July | Withdraw a course with a W online                               |
| 5 July            | Final exam seating plans go up                                  |
| 5 to 16 July      | Withdraw with a W through the faculty, with the dean's approval |
| 12 to 15 July     | Last day of the summer session, depending on your section       |
| 17 to 20 July     | Final exams                                                     |

Your section decides when the summer session ends. Sections 8123 and 8245 finish on 12 July 2027,
and sections 3883 and 5984 finish on 15 July 2027.

## Deadlines for resolving an I grade

The faculty must send outstanding results to the Registrar by these dates.

| Deadline          | Applies to                                         |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| 20 January 2027   | Semester 1, students admitted in 2568 or later     |
| 19 February 2027  | Semester 1, students admitted before 2568          |
| 30 June 2027      | Semester 2, students admitted in 2568 or later     |
| 30 July 2027      | Semester 2, students admitted before 2568          |
| 13 September 2027 | Summer session, students admitted in 2568 or later |
| 13 October 2027   | Summer session, students admitted before 2568      |

Dates can change. Follow BIRSA's channels for updates, and watch for BIRSA's separate announcement
of BIR's own registration and payment dates.
