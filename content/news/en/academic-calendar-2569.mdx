Thammasat University's Registrar has published the academic calendar for AY 2569 (2026/2027). Here are the university-wide dates that apply to BIR students.

**Course registration and tuition payment for BIR are announced separately by BIRSA.** BIR is a special programme, so its registration and payment windows are not the same as the regular-programme dates in the Registrar's calendar. Do not use the regular-programme windows for your own registration or payment.

## Semester 1 (August to December 2026)

- 3 August: Semester 1 begins
- 3 to 16 August: Late registration and add-drop
- 3 to 16 August: Apply to graduate
- 3 to 17 August: Payment deadline for late registration and add-drop
- 18 August: Late-add penalty fees begin
- 19 August to 12 October: Withdraw a course with a W (online)
- 1 September: Last day to register or take leave before your name is removed
- 14 September: Midterm seating plans available
- 20 to 27 September: Midterm exam period
- 13 October to 22 November: Withdraw with a W through the faculty (dean's approval)
- 16 November: Final exam seating plans available
- 21 November: Last day of semester 1
- 23 November to 11 December: Final exam period

The final exam period is not continuous. Exams run 23 to 30 November, then 1 to 4, 8 to 9 and 11 December 2026.

## Semester 2 (January to May 2027)

- 4 January: Semester 2 begins
- 4 to 17 January: Late registration and add-drop
- 4 to 17 January: Apply to graduate
- 4 to 18 January: Payment deadline for late registration and add-drop
- 18 January to 14 March: Withdraw a course with a W (online)
- 19 January: Late-add penalty fees begin
- 2 February: Last day to register or take leave before your name is removed
- 15 February: Midterm seating plans available
- 21 to 28 February: Midterm exam period
- 15 March to 2 May: Withdraw with a W through the faculty (dean's approval)
- 12 to 18 April: No classes for Songkran
- 19 April: Classes resume
- 26 April: Final exam seating plans available
- 1 May: Last day of semester 2
- 3 to 18 May: Final exam period

The final exam period is not continuous either. Exams run on 3 May, then 5 to 18 May 2027.

## Summer session (June to July 2027)

- 7 June: Summer session begins
- 7 to 13 June: Late registration and add-drop
- 7 to 13 June: Apply to graduate
- 7 to 14 June: Payment deadline for late registration and add-drop
- 15 June: Late-add penalty fees begin
- 16 June to 4 July: Withdraw a course with a W (online)
- 5 July: Final exam seating plans available
- 5 to 16 July: Withdraw with a W through the faculty (dean's approval)
- 12 to 15 July: Last day of the summer session
- 17 to 20 July: Final exam period

The summer session's last day depends on your section: 12 July 2027 for sections 8123 and 8245, and 15 July 2027 for sections 3883 and 5984.

## Deadlines for resolving an I grade

The faculty must submit outstanding results to the Registrar by these dates.

- 20 January 2027: Semester 1, students admitted 2568 onwards
- 19 February 2027: Semester 1, students admitted before 2568
- 30 June 2027: Semester 2, students admitted 2568 onwards
- 30 July 2027: Semester 2, students admitted before 2568
- 13 September 2027: Summer, students admitted 2568 onwards
- 13 October 2027: Summer, students admitted before 2568

Dates are subject to change. Follow BIRSA's channels for updates, and watch for BIRSA's separate announcement of BIR's own registration and payment dates.
