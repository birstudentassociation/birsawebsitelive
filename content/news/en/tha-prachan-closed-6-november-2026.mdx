The Faculty of Political Science, all Thammasat libraries and the Tha Prachan campus are closed on Friday 6 November 2026. The closure is for the royal barge procession on the Chao Phraya.

<WarningText title="Do not travel to Tha Prachan on 6 November 2026">
  The faculty, all libraries and the whole campus are closed for the day. All classes are online.
</WarningText>

## What this means for you

- All classes on 6 November run online.
- You cannot enter the campus, the faculty building or any library.
- Your lecturers will tell you how to join each class. Check your course channels and email.

## Library books and BIR announcements

Return any library books on the next day the libraries open. Plan any study that needs library
access around the closure.

The BIR office announces changes to BIR classes on [birpolsci.com](https://www.birpolsci.com) and
on the BIR Program Facebook page. Read the Thammasat University announcements for the full notice.

## The procession

The Royal Thai Navy holds the royal barge procession on the Chao Phraya on 6 November 2026.
Bangkok has named 16 public viewing points along both banks. The river and the roads near the
campus will be busy, so expect delays if you are in the area.
