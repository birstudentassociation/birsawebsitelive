BIR Student Association (BIRSA) invites all students to join Singhadang Samphan
(โครงการสิงห์แดงสัมพันธ์), its annual Red Lion relations event, for AY 2569 (2026/2027). This
year's cohort is Singhadang 78.

| Date                       | Stage                                             |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Now to 30 June 2026, 23:59 | Registration open                                 |
| 31 July 2026               | Event day at Thammasat University, Rangsit campus |

Use your Dome email (@dome.tu.ac.th) to complete the registration form. Register through the
Google Form before the deadline.
