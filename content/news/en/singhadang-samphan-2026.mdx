BIRSA invites all students to join "Singhadang Samphan" (โครงการสิงห์แดงสัมพันธ์) for academic year 2026 — the annual Red Lion relations event. This year's cohort is Singhadang 78.

Registration is open now until 30 June 2026, 23:59. Use your Dome (@dome.tu.ac.th) email to complete the registration form. The event itself takes place on 31 July 2026 at Thammasat University, Rangsit Campus.

Don't miss it — register through the link below before the deadline.
