The Faculty of Political Science is welcoming its newest undergraduates, the 2026 intake (code 69), with a full-day orientation at Thammasat University's Rangsit campus. If you're joining the BIR programme, this is the faculty-wide welcome that runs alongside BIRSA's own [BIR18 orientation](/en/news/bir18-orientation-schedule).

## What to expect

- Meet the faculty, your fellow first-years, and the people you'll spend the next four years with.
- Get your bearings on campus before teaching starts, including a look at Rangsit's Social Sciences building.
- Learn how faculty life, activities, and student groups like BIRSA fit together.

Come ready for a full day. The programme runs from morning registration through to the late afternoon.
