**Seniors or Bosses?** is an open forum on SOTUS with students from South Korea. It runs from
16:30 to 18:30 on **Monday 9 November 2026** in Room 103, Faculty of Political Science.

SOTUS stands for Seniority, Order, Tradition, Unity and Spirit. It is the system of senior and
junior rules that shapes freshers' activities at many Thai universities. Korean campuses have their
own version. The forum asks how the two compare, and where respect for seniors ends and taking
orders from them begins.
