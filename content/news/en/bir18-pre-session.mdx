The BIR18 Pre-Session runs on 22 July 2026 from 08:30 to 15:00 in Room R.102 at the Faculty of Political Science, Thammasat University, Tha Prachan campus.

The day gets incoming students ready for the year ahead through three talks, with breaks in between.

Programme schedule:

- 08:30 to 09:00: Registration
- 09:00 to 10:30: Introduction to "College Survival", with Dr. Wasin Punthong
- 10:30 to 10:40: Refreshment break
- 10:40 to 12:10: Introduction to "Academic Integrity", with Assoc. Prof. Dr. Peera Charoenvattananukul
- 12:10 to 13:30: Lunch break
- 13:30 to 15:00: Introduction to "Etiquette and Netiquette", with Dr. Purawich Watanasukh

The schedule is subject to change as appropriate.
