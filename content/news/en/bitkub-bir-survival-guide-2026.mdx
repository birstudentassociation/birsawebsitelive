## The speakers

### 🤝 Partnerships in Practice, Managing Relationships and Growing Career

**Tawichat Taneerat (P'Utah, BMIR8)** is Senior Business Partnerships Manager at Bitkub. The session covers

- what business development and partnership management involve day to day
- how to build trust with stakeholders at every level
- negotiating for a result that works for both sides
- handling conflict and goals that do not line up, professionally

### 🏛️ Inside Government Affairs, Business, Public Policy and Stakeholder Management

**Phuri Songprasert (Phuri, BIR16)** is a Government Affairs Intern at Bitkub. The session covers

- government affairs as a career for political science graduates
- how the role links company goals, regulators and public policy
- stakeholder mapping to find who holds influence in each sector
- building long-term trust through transparency and good information

### 💼 Behind the Hiring Desk, What Corporate HR Really Looks For

**Kullapat Wiwaiwuthphinyo (P'Yaya, BMIR1)** is People Director at Bitkub. The session covers

- how HR is organised, including talent acquisition and HR business partners
- the CV mistakes that get an application rejected straight away
- setting up a LinkedIn profile with the keywords recruiters search for
- what common screening questions are really testing
