The future of BIR18 starts with you. BIR Student Association (BIRSA) has announced the timeline for the BIR18 Student Council election, and every BIR18 first-year student is eligible to apply.

## Candidate requirements

- Must be currently studying in the BIR program as a first-year student, Batch 18 (ID 69).

## Positions available

- BIR18 Class President (1 position)
- Rangsit Coordinator (1 position)
- Tha Prachan Coordinator (1 position)
- Other Committee Members (no more than 3 positions)

## Student Council role and responsibility

- **Planning and organizing:** Coordinating and taking responsibility for BIR18's activities.
- **Advocacy:** Representing and voicing suggestions regarding BIR18 students, and proposing ideas on how the council runs.
- **Collaboration:** Cooperating with BIRSA and other first-year (BIR18) councils as deemed appropriate.
- **Networking:** Working with the Faculty of Political Science's Class 78 Student Council to share information and organize joint activities.

The BIR18 council should also prepare some of its members to help run joint activities with the Class 78 Student Council at the Rangsit campus.

## Election timeline

| Date | Stage |
| --- | --- |
| 10 to 14 August 2026 | Application period |
| 16 August 2026 | Formal candidates declared |
| 16 to 24 August 2026 | Election campaign |
| 19 August 2026 | Policy statement |
| 24 August 2026 | Election day |

## Election rules

Candidates and their parties may not: use threats, intimidation, or violence against voters; give money or gifts to win votes (ordinary campaign materials like posters and banners are exempt); insult or defame candidates from other parties; spread false claims about other candidates; damage election property or another candidate's property; or coordinate campaigning with parties running for the Faculty's Class 78 Student Council election. Violations are handled with a warning first, escalating to disqualification for repeat or serious breaches.

## How to apply

1. Download the application form from [BIRSA's Google Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Eca6gzy3GKUASeugWrTYY4uRwSDMQav4?usp=sharing). The QR code on BIRSA's Instagram announcement goes to the same form.
2. Submit your completed form by email to [birstudentassociation@gmail.com](mailto:birstudentassociation@gmail.com). Your application counts as submitted only once BIRSA has received it.

Deadline: 14 August 2026.
