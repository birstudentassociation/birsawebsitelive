The future of BIR18 starts with you. BIR Student Association (BIRSA) has set the timeline for the
BIR18 Student Council election, and every BIR18 first-year student can apply.

## Who can apply

You can apply if you are currently studying in the BIR programme as a first-year student in Batch 18
(ID 69).

## Positions available

- BIR18 Class President (1 position)
- Rangsit Coordinator (1 position)
- Tha Prachan Coordinator (1 position)
- Other Committee Members (no more than 3 positions)

## What the council does

- Plans BIR18's activities and takes responsibility for running them.
- Represents BIR18 students, carries their suggestions forward, and proposes how the council itself should work.
- Works with BIRSA and the other year groups' councils wherever that makes sense.
- Works with the Faculty of Political Science's Class 78 Student Council to share information and run joint activities.

The council should also set aside a few of its members to help run joint activities with the Class
78 Student Council at the Rangsit campus.

## Election timeline

| Date                 | Stage                      |
| -------------------- | -------------------------- |
| 10 to 14 August 2026 | Application period         |
| 16 August 2026       | Formal candidates declared |
| 16 to 24 August 2026 | Election campaign          |
| 19 August 2026       | Policy statement           |
| 24 August 2026       | Election day               |

## Election rules

Candidates and their parties must not

- threaten, intimidate, or use violence against voters
- give money or gifts to win votes, though ordinary campaign materials such as posters and banners are fine
- insult or defame candidates from other parties
- spread false claims about other candidates
- damage election property or another candidate's property
- campaign jointly with parties running in the Faculty's Class 78 Student Council election

BIRSA warns candidates first, and disqualifies anyone who repeats a breach or commits a serious one.

## How to apply

1. Download the application form from [BIRSA's Google Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Eca6gzy3GKUASeugWrTYY4uRwSDMQav4?usp=sharing). The QR code on BIRSA's Instagram announcement goes to the same form.
2. Email your completed form to [birstudentassociation@gmail.com](mailto:birstudentassociation@gmail.com). Your application counts as submitted only once BIRSA has received it.

Applications close on 14 August 2026.
