The future of BIR18 starts with you. BIR Student Association (BIRSA) is opening applications for the BIR18 Student Council, and every BIR18 first-year student is eligible to apply.

<Notice>Applications close 14 August 2026. Submit your application form by email.</Notice>

## Candidate requirements

- Must be currently studying in the BIR program as a first-year student, Batch 18 (ID 69).

## Positions available

- BIR 18 Class President (1 position)
- Rangsit Coordinator (1 position)
- Tha Prachan Coordinator (1 position)
- Other Committee Members (no more than 3 positions)

## Student Council role and responsibility

- **Planning and organizing:** Coordinating and taking responsibility for BIR18's activities.
- **Advocacy:** Representing and voicing suggestions regarding BIR18 students.
- **Collaboration:** Cooperating with BIRSA and others as deemed appropriate.
- **Networking:** Working with the Class 76 Student Council to organize joint activities.

## How to apply

1. Get the application form (linked from the original Instagram post).
2. Submit your completed form by email to [birstudentassociation@gmail.com](mailto:birstudentassociation@gmail.com).

Deadline: now through 14 August 2026.
