The Faculty of Political Science has appointed the BIR18 Student Council for AY 2569 (2026/2027).
The BIR Student Association (BIRSA) ran the election, and all six BIR18 Rising Star candidates now
take up their posts. Congratulations to the new council.

## The BIR18 Student Council

The Faculty published the names in Thai, so they appear here as announced.

| Name                    | Position                |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| จีรณา แสงสว่าง          | BIR18 Class President   |
| วิชญ์ เอี่ยมวุฒิกร      | Rangsit Coordinator     |
| พิมพ์มาดา ตังอนุสรณ์สุข | Tha Prachan Coordinator |
| วรัญญา มณีธรรม          | Public Relations        |
| ตะวันฉาย อุตมงค์        | Activities              |
| วิศวกร วงศ์พิภพมงคล     | Academic Affairs        |

## The announcement

Associate Professor Dr Puli Fuwongcharoen, Dean of the Faculty of Political Science, signed the
announcement on 1 September 2026.

To contact the council, message [@bir18.risingstar](https://www.instagram.com/bir18.risingstar/) on
Instagram. The candidates' policies and the election timeline are in
[BIR18 Rising Star candidates present their policies on 19 August](/en/news/bir18-student-council-candidates-2026).
