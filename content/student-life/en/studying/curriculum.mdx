## Credits by cohort

| Cohorts                                                      | General education | Major | Free electives | Total   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------- | ----- | -------------- | ------- |
| 64 to 67 (Curriculum 2021, B.E. 2564, and its 2023 revision) | 30                | 91    | 6              | **127** |
| 68 and 69 (Curriculum B.E. 2568)                             | 30                | 90    | 6              | **126** |

The first digits of your student ID give your cohort. Cohorts 64 and 65 follow the original 2564 handout and cohorts 66 and 67 follow the 2023 revision, which replaced some Year 1 general education courses. The course lists on this page are those of the 2023 revision. The 2568 curriculum uses the same course codes, with the differences set out under [Curriculum 2568](#curriculum-2568).

<Notice variant="info" title="Plan your own degree">

  courses you have already passed.
</Notice>

## General education courses

General education is 30 credits in two parts.

| Part                                              | Credits |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| Part 1, Thammasat University requirements         | 21      |
| Part 2, Faculty of Political Science requirements | 9       |

### Part 1 (21 credits)

Social Sciences

- TU100 Civil Education
- TU101 Thailand, ASEAN and the World

Humanities

- TU102 Life & Aesthetics

Science and Mathematics

- TU103 Life and Sustainability

Languages

- EL105 English Communication Skills
- LAS101 Critical Thinking, Reading and Writing
- TU106 Creativity and Communication

### Part 2 (9 credits)

- PI121 Introduction to Social Science
- PI122 Introduction to Humanities
- One course from AH208 Exercise for Good Health and Well-Being or EL295 Academic English and Study Skill 1

Your English exemption result and each semester's timetable can change which general education courses you take and when.

## Four-year study plan

Summer terms are optional, except the Year 3 internship summer.

- PI272 Foreign Policy Analysis
- PI292 International Organizations and Regimes

### Year 2, summer (optional)

In Year 4, semester 2, you take the required courses you have not yet taken. There is no fixed list, so plan this semester with your academic adviser.

## Curriculum 2568

Cohorts 68 and 69 follow the 2568 curriculum, which is 126 credits (30 general education, 90 major, 6 free electives). The course codes are the same as in the lists above, with these differences.

- PI574 is a 3-credit free elective and is optional. It can count towards the 6 free elective credits. The plan has no compulsory Year 3 summer internship term.
- General education is 30 credits grouped by theme, not split into a university part and a faculty part.
- The Year 2 summer term is part of the plan and is not marked optional.

The 2568 plan is as follows.

| Term               | Courses                                                                                                                                                      |
