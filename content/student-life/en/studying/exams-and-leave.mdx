## Missing an exam

If you cannot attend an exam for a reason you could not avoid, you or someone you appoint submits a petition to the course instructor. Do this as soon as possible and keep evidence, such as a medical certificate.

If the petition is approved, you either withdraw from the course and receive a W, or the instructor assesses you in another way. If it is not approved, the instructor assesses you on the work you have already submitted.

The earlier rules gave a deadline of 10 days after the exam and sent the petition to the Dean through the instructor. Check the current deadline and route with the BIR office on 02-221-6111 ext. 3409 before you submit.

## Leave of absence

You request leave from the Dean and give a reason. The route depends on when you ask.

| When you ask                                                    | What happens                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Before you register, within 30 days of the semester opening     | Leave is recorded for the semester. You pay the fee to maintain student status.                           |
| After you register, within 14 days of the opening (7 in summer) | Your courses are deleted from your record, and you pay the fee to maintain student status.                |
| Later in the semester, up to week 10 (week 4 in summer)         | Your courses are recorded as W. Ask the BIR office first, because approval may be needed beyond the Dean. |

If you neither register nor request leave within 30 days of the semester opening, you are dismissed. See [Registering, adding and dropping courses](/student-life/studying/registration).

Earlier rules said that first-year students could not take leave in their first two semesters, and that leave beyond two consecutive semesters needed the Rector's approval. These rules are not confirmed in the 2568 regulation. Ask the BIR office before you plan a long leave.

### Leave and the time limit

Leave counts as study time. It does not extend the 8-year maximum. See [Grades, GPA and graduating](/student-life/studying/grades-and-graduation).

## Suspension

Suspension is a disciplinary penalty, and it is different from leave. You pay the fee to maintain student status while suspended.

- If the suspension starts in a later semester and you have already paid tuition and fees, you are refunded them and pay the fee to maintain student status instead.
- If the suspension starts in the current semester, all your enrolled courses are deleted from your record.
- Suspension counts towards the 8-year maximum.
