The internship for students with ID 67 lasts 8 weeks. It is the course PI574 Internship in Politics, Public Administration and International Relations, in the summer session of academic year 2569 (2026/2027).

The internship is optional. If you complete it, you receive 3 credits, which count as 1 free elective course.

You find the organisation yourself. The BIR office issues the letters the organisation needs.

## Step by step

<InternshipSteps />

To get the credit, you must attend at least 2 training sessions, submit each form on time, and register and pay for PI574.

For security reasons, direct links to the forms and some other internship materials are available only in the Internship 2026 (ID67) channel on Microsoft Teams.

## Which letter you need

There are 2 letters. Everyone on the internship needs the Letter of Internship. You need the Letter of Internship Request only while you are still applying.

<InternshipLetters />

## Letter of Internship Request

The Letter of Internship Request (หนังสือขอความอนุเคราะห์รับนักศึกษาฝึกงาน) asks an organisation to consider you for an internship. It confirms that you are a student and that the internship is part of your programme. It gives your name, the internship period and the objectives of the internship.

If an organisation has already offered you a place, you do not need it.

The BIR office does not post or deliver letters. Collect yours from the office.

### Applying to more than 1 organisation

You can apply to only 1 organisation at a time, and each request is for 1 organisation. You can submit the Internship Request Form up to 2 times.

For a second letter, you must show that the first organisation turned you down, or that you withdrew your application to it properly.

## Letter of Internship

<WarningText title="Every student on the internship needs a Letter of Internship">
  Submit the Secured Internship Form with your proof of acceptance by the end of April 2027.
</WarningText>

The Letter of Internship (หนังสือส่งตัวเข้าฝึกงาน) confirms your placement. The faculty issues it after it approves your placement.

The letter tells the organisation that you are on the BIR internship programme. It gives the organisation the faculty's contact details and details of the programme. The faculty and the organisation use it to coordinate and monitor your internship, and BIR uses it to assess the internship for credit.

Proof of acceptance can be an acceptance email, an offer letter, a confirmation letter or another official message from the organisation.

## Register and pay for PI574

Register for PI574 in the summer session of academic year 2569 on [reg.tu.ac.th](https://reg.tu.ac.th). Pay the fee by the deadline Thammasat University announces.
