The internship for students with ID 67 runs for 8 weeks, from 7 June to 30 July 2027. It is the course PI574 in the summer session of academic year 2569 (2026/2027).

You find the organisation yourself. The BIR office issues the letters the organisation needs.

## Dates and deadlines

| What                                                                | When                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Internship Request Form (Form 1), only if you need a request letter | By the end of March 2027 |
| Secured Internship Form (Form 2), required for everyone             | By the end of April 2027 |
| Internship orientation, where you collect your Letter of Internship | May 2027                 |
| Internship                                                          | 7 June to 30 July 2027   |

## What you must do to get the credit

To complete the internship, you must

- attend at least 2 training sessions run by BIR and the Faculty of Political Science
- submit each form on time and follow the steps for both letters
- register for PI574 and pay the fee by the University's deadline

PI574 is 3 credits. If you complete the internship, the credits count as 1 free elective course. Check [the BIR curriculum](/student-life/studying/curriculum) for whether your curriculum requires the internship.

## Step by step

1. Attend the Happy Hour session. Then look for internships in the areas that interest you.
2. If an organisation asks for a letter from the faculty, submit the Internship Request Form by the end of March 2027.
3. Attend at least 2 training sessions. BIR checks attendance at each one.
4. Once an organisation accepts you, submit the Secured Internship Form with your proof of acceptance by the end of April 2027.
5. Collect your Letter of Internship at the internship orientation in May 2027.
6. Register for PI574 and pay the fee.
7. Give the Letter of Internship to the organisation on your first day.

The links to both forms are posted in the Internship 2026 (ID67) team on Microsoft Teams.

## Letter of Internship Request

The Letter of Internship Request (หนังสือขอความอนุเคราะห์รับนักศึกษาฝึกงาน) asks an organisation to consider you for an internship. It confirms that you are a student and that the internship is part of your programme. It gives your name, the internship period and the objectives of the internship.

Send it to the organisation with your application. You need it only while you are applying. If an organisation has already offered you a place, you do not need it.

### How to get the request letter

1. Submit the Internship Request Form by the end of March 2027.
2. Upload information about the organisation and your CV with the form.
3. Collect the letter from the BIR office after 5 to 7 working days.

The BIR office does not post or deliver letters.

### Applying to more than 1 organisation

You can apply to only 1 organisation at a time, and each request is for 1 organisation. You can submit the Internship Request Form up to 2 times.

For a second letter, you must show that the first organisation turned you down, or that you withdrew your application to it properly.

## Letter of Internship

<WarningText title="Every student on the internship needs a Letter of Internship">
  Submit the Secured Internship Form with your proof of acceptance by the end of April 2027.
</WarningText>

The Letter of Internship (หนังสือส่งตัวเข้าฝึกงาน) confirms your placement. The faculty issues it after it approves your placement.

The letter tells the organisation that you are on the BIR internship programme. It gives the organisation the faculty's contact details and details of the programme. The faculty and the organisation use it to monitor your internship, and BIR uses it to assess the internship for credit.

### How to get the Letter of Internship

1. Get an offer or acceptance from the organisation.
2. Keep your proof of acceptance. This can be an acceptance email, an offer letter, a confirmation letter or another official message from the organisation.
3. Submit the Secured Internship Form and upload your proof by the end of April 2027.
4. Collect the letter at the internship orientation in May 2027.
5. Give the letter to the organisation on your first day.

<WarningText title="You cannot switch organisations freely">
  Once an organisation has accepted you, you cannot change or cancel without valid reasons and the programme's approval.

If you intern somewhere else instead, you must tell the organisation that accepted you and give your reasons. The internship will not be credited or graded.

</WarningText>

## How the internship is marked

<InsetText>
  These criteria were published for students with ID 66. The BIR office has not yet published the
  criteria for ID 67.
</InsetText>

The internship is graded S (satisfactory) or U (unsatisfactory). It does not use the grade point scale.

### Marking criteria

The internship is marked out of 100, split evenly between the programme and your workplace supervisor.

| Marked by            | Component                                    | Marks |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| BIR (50)             | Internship supervision (focus group or Zoom) | 5     |
| BIR (50)             | Internship report                            | 45    |
| Your supervisor (50) | First evaluation                             | 20    |
| Your supervisor (50) | Final evaluation                             | 30    |

If the first evaluation is below 10 marks, the lecturer in charge meets you and your supervisor to discuss your responsibilities and performance.

### Internship report

The report is worth 45 marks.

- Length is 12 to 15 pages, not counting tables, charts and pictures.
- Font is Times New Roman 12 point, single spaced.

It covers four things.

1. The organisation's structure, objectives and tasks
2. A weekly journal
3. A self-assessment of what you gained, your strengths and your weaknesses
4. How the work links to the theories and approaches you have studied
