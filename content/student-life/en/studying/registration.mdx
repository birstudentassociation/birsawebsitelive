## How to register

You register online at the Registrar's Office website, [reg.tu.ac.th](https://reg.tu.ac.th), or in the TU Greats app. The Registrar's Office sets the registration dates for each semester.

The BIR programme posts each term's course list on [birpolsci.com](https://www.birpolsci.com) and on its Facebook page, BIR Program. Choose your courses from that list, then register within the announced period.

## Credit limits

You can register for at most 22 credits in a regular semester and 6 credits in the summer session. The Dean can approve more credits for a student who is about to graduate.

The earlier rules also set a minimum of 9 credits a semester. The 2568 regulation does not state a minimum, so check with the BIR office before you register for fewer than 9.

## Adding a course

You can add a course in the first 14 days of a regular semester, or the first 7 days of the summer session. The course instructor must approve it.

After that period the Dean can approve a late addition. A daily late fee applies, and the last possible day is the final day of the semester.

## Dropping a course

A course you drop in the first 14 days of a regular semester (7 days in summer) does not appear on your academic record.

If you drop a course and do not replace it, you can ask for a refund of its tuition. Send the refund form and your TU REG withdrawal proof to <Email address="bir@tu.ac.th" /> within 7 days of dropping the course. See [Tuition, fees and refunds](/student-life/money/tuition-and-fees) for the fee rules.

## Withdrawing from a course

After the drop period you can still withdraw during the first 10 weeks of a regular semester, or the first 4 weeks of the summer session. The course stays on your record with the letter W (withdrawn).

After the withdrawal period only the Dean can approve a withdrawal.

## If you do not register

If you neither register nor request leave within 30 days of the semester opening, you are dismissed from the University. If you cannot register, ask for leave first. See [Missing an exam, leave and suspension](/student-life/studying/exams-and-leave).

## Failed courses

There are no re-examinations. Retake a failed course when it is next offered.
