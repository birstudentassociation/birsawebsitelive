## Grades

Thammasat uses this grade scale.

| Grade | Points |
| ----- | ------ |
| A     | 4.0    |
| B+    | 3.5    |
| B     | 3.0    |
| C+    | 2.5    |
| C     | 2.0    |
| D+    | 1.5    |
| D     | 1.0    |
| F     | 0      |

Each course sets how it is assessed, and the syllabus states the mix. It can include a mid-term, a final exam, papers, presentations and class participation.

Attendance is required throughout the semester, and each course sets its own attendance rule. Read the syllabus or ask the instructor in the first week.

Courses graded S (satisfactory) or U (unsatisfactory) do not count towards your GPA. Every other grade counts towards your cumulative GPA, which is the average across all your semesters, summer sessions included.

## Degree requirements

You are nominated for the Bachelor of Political Science when you have completed all curriculum requirements with a cumulative GPA of at least 2.00. The number of credits depends on your cohort.

| Cohort                       | Credits | Made up of                                      |
| ---------------------------- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| ID 64 to 67                  | 127     | 30 general education, 91 major, 6 free elective |
| 2568 curriculum (from ID 68) | 126     | 30 general education, 90 major, 6 free elective |

Ask the BIR office which curriculum applies to you. See [The BIR curriculum and study plan](/student-life/studying/curriculum) for the courses.

Earlier rules also required at least 7 semesters of study. The 2568 regulation does not state this, so check with the BIR office if you plan to finish early.

### Asking to graduate

Submit a written request to graduate within the first 10 days of your final semester (7 in summer). Check the date with the Registrar, because the date is set each semester.

### Time limit

The longest you can study is twice the standard length of the programme, which is 8 years for a four-year degree. Leave and suspension count towards the 8 years.

## Honours

### First class honours

You need all of the following.

1. You complete the curriculum within 4 academic years. Leave and approved study or training of up to one year do not count towards the 4 years.
2. Your cumulative GPA is at least 3.50.
3. You have never received a grade below C, and never a U.
4. You have never repeated a course or received an F.
5. You have never received a disciplinary penalty of probation (ทัณฑ์บน) or a more serious penalty.
6. Credits transferred or exempted from another programme are no more than 25% of your total.

### Second class honours

There are two routes. Both require points 1, 4, 5 and 6 above and no U grade. In addition, choose one route.

1. Your cumulative GPA is at least 3.50, you have received a grade below C in some course, and your GPA in major courses is at least 2.00.
2. Your cumulative GPA is at least 3.25 and you have never received a grade below C in a major course.
