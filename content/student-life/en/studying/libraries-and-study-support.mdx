## Your faculty library

The Direk Jayanama Library (ห้องสมุด ศ.ดิเรก ชัยนาม) is on floor 5 of the Faculty of Political Science. It opens Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 19:00 and closes on Saturday, Sunday and public holidays.

The other faculty libraries keep different hours.

## Pridi Banomyong Library

Pridi Banomyong Library (หอสมุดปรีดี พนมยงค์) is open every day from 08:30 to 21:30, except public holidays. Call 02-221-6111 ext. 7304 or email <Email address="pridilibrary@tu.ac.th" />.

It has study rooms, a co-learning space, power sockets and phone chargers throughout, and blankets to borrow. Staff can help you find books in Thai and English.

## Borrowing books

As an undergraduate you can borrow up to 25 books at a time, for 30 days.

- Book Delivery moves a book between Thammasat library branches, so you do not need to travel to the branch that holds it.
- Full Text Finder searches for articles, journals, e-books and theses.

## Booking a study room

Pridi Banomyong Library rooms for reading, working and meetings, and its film room, are booked through the library's LINE official account.

1. Add the library on LINE. Search for @Lifeonline or scan the library's QR code.
2. Choose the Booking menu, then choose to book a room.
3. Choose the room and the time slot.
4. Confirm the booking in LINE.

The film room has streaming services.

## Library of Things

The library lends equipment against your student card, either the physical card or the TU Greats App. Items include laptops, iPads, cameras, extension leads and board games.

## Free printing

Every student has a free printing quota of 100 baht each semester. You can use it at these places.

- Pridi Banomyong Library, floor U2
- The computer room on floor 2 of Puey Ungphakorn Library

Both libraries are on the Tha Prachan campus.

## Research and writing support

Turnitin checks your own work for plagiarism before you submit it. The free AI checker is explained in [Plagiarism and AI use](/student-life/studying/plagiarism).

The library also runs U-Services at [userservice.library.tu.ac.th/u-services](https://userservice.library.tu.ac.th/u-services/).

- English abstract editing. Upload your abstract and a language specialist edits it. You get it back by email within three working days. Call 02-613-3546 or email <Email address="tuabstractediting@gmail.com" />.
- Here to Help answers questions about the library's services and research tools.
- A Freepik and Flaticon service provides images and icons for coursework.

## Computer Service Center

The Tha Prachan Computer Service Center is in the gymnasium building. It is open Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 19:00.

## TU-GET

TU-GET is Thammasat's own English proficiency test. Results can support an application for credit exemption and can be compared with TOEFL iBT and IELTS scores. Check with the BIR office whether your curriculum requires a TU-GET score.

|             | TU-GET PBT (paper)                         | TU-GET CBT (computer)                                 |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Format      | Multiple choice on paper                   | On a computer, all four skills                        |
| Sections    | Structure, vocabulary and reading          | Reading, Listening, Speaking, Writing (30 marks each) |
| Total score | 1,000                                      | 120                                                   |
| Test centre | Language Institute building, Tha Prachan   | Language Institute test centre, floor 1, SC1, Rangsit |
| Fee         | 800 baht (1,000 baht if you register late) | 1,500 baht (1,800 baht if you register late)          |
| Results     | About 5 to 7 days                          | About 15 days                                         |

Scores are valid for two years. Fees change, so check the current fee and register at [litu.tu.ac.th/testing/tu-get](https://litu.tu.ac.th/testing/tu-get/).
