## Contact the BIR office

The BIR office is the Faculty's own office for the programme. It handles admissions, registration questions, records and official letters. It is separate from BIRSA, the student association that runs this website.

- Phone 02-221-6111 ext. 3409
- Email <Email address="bir@tu.ac.th" />
- Open Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 16:00

The office is in the Faculty of Political Science building. The address is BIR Program, Faculty of Political Science, Thammasat University, Tha Prachan Campus, 2 Prachan Road, Bangkok 10200.

Exchange students contact the exchange coordinator at oiapolsc@tu.ac.th or on ext. 3403.

## The BIR programme

BIR is the international programme of the Faculty of Political Science. Its full name is the Bachelor of Political Science (Politics and International Relations) (International Program). Classes are in English.

Some classes have guest lecturers. The programme includes a field trip and a compulsory internship. See [the BIR curriculum](/student-life/studying/curriculum) and [internship](/student-life/studying/internship).

## The Faculty of Political Science

The Faculty was established in 1949. It teaches undergraduate and graduate degrees in Politics and Government, Public Administration, and International Relations. Graduate programmes run full time for regular students and part time for working executives.

## Thammasat University

Pridi Banomyong founded Thammasat on 27 June 1934 as the University of Moral and Political Sciences. He wanted to teach the Thai public about democracy, which Thailand had adopted in 1932.

The philosophy of the university is reflected in a speech by Pridi.

> "A university is, figuratively, an oasis that quenches the thirst of those who are in pursuit of knowledge. The opportunity to acquire higher education rightly belong to every citizen under the principle of freedom of education."

After the coup of 8 November 1947, the university dropped its open admission system and offered four degrees in Law, Political Science, Economics, and Commerce and Accountancy. The name Thammasat University came with the Thammasat University Act of 1952 (B.E. 2495), which required each programme to award its own degree.

In 2011 staff and students came together to help communities in need, in the spirit of the university motto, "I love Thammasat because Thammasat teaches me to love the people."
