## How to join

Message the club through its Instagram account, or go to a BIRSA event. BIR students can join clubs at any level, in BIR, at Tha Prachan or across Thammasat.

## BIR clubs

BIR students run 12 clubs. See the [BIR club directory](/clubs) for how to contact each one.

- ASA IR runs student volunteer camps.
- BIRify is a podcast and Kien Club is a writers' club.
- The music club is BIR Music Club.
- The sport and games clubs are BIR Basketball, BIR Football, BIR Volleyball, BIR Esports Club and BIR CardGame Club.
- Three clubs run simulations. TU MUN simulates United Nations bodies, Parliamock.TU simulates the Thai Parliament and BIR Mock Fund (IAIC) simulates an investment fund.

## BIRSA events

BIRSA announces dates and sign-ups on [News](/news) and on Instagram at [@student_birsa](https://instagram.com/student_birsa).

- BIR the Future is a camp for high school students who may join BIR.
- BIR Open House is for high school students and parents deciding on BIR.
- BIR the Newbies welcomes incoming first years before the semester starts.
- BIR Byenior is a farewell event for graduating students. All year groups may go.
- BIR Movie Day, BIR Seminar and BIR Sports run through the year.

See [BIR events](/activity/events) for more detail.

## Volunteering

- ASA IR and the Tha Prachan Volunteer Club run volunteer camps. Follow their Instagram accounts for applications.
- BIRSA opens sub-committee recruitment each year and announces it on [News](/news). Many BIRSA activities also rely on volunteers and short term helpers around specific events. To help before a recruitment opens, contact a current committee member through [Contact BIRSA](/contact).
