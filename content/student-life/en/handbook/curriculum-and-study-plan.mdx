<Notice variant="info" title="About this chapter">
This chapter reproduces the 2023 revision of Curriculum 2021 (B.E. 2564), the BIR Academic Handout that sets out the current course structure and study plan. The exact general education courses you take, and the order you take them in, can vary depending on your English-exemption result and each term's course arrangement.
</Notice>

## Curriculum structure

The BIR programme requires a minimum of 127 credits, made up as follows.

| Component | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| General education courses | 30 |
| Major requirements | 91 |
| Free elective courses | 6 |
| **Total** | **127** |

General education courses are split into two parts:

| Part | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Part 1 (Thammasat University requirements) | 21 |
| Part 2 (Faculty of Political Science requirements) | 9 |

## General education courses

### Part 1 (21 credits)

You must complete the following courses in accordance with the requirements of Thammasat University.

Sociology:

- TU100 Civic Engagement
- TU101 Thailand, ASEAN and the World

Anthropology:

- TU102 Social Life Skills

Science and Mathematics:

- TU103 Life and Sustainability

Languages:

- EL105 English Communication Skills
- LAS101 Critical Thinking, Reading and Writing
- TU106 Creativity and Communication

### Part 2 (9 credits)

You must complete the following courses in accordance with the requirements of the Faculty of Political Science.

- PD102 Social Sciences in the 21st Century
- PD103 Humanities in the Age of Disruption
- Select one course from: AH208 Exercise for Good Health and Well-Being, or EL295 Academic English and Study Skill 1

## Minor courses

Minor Courses make up 21 credits of your major requirements. You'll choose one of the following three minors. Each minor has Required Courses (9 credits), Elective Courses within the minor (select 2 courses, 6 credits), and Elective Courses in other minors (6 credits).

## Free electives

You must select at least 6 credits of Free Elective Courses from any courses offered by Thammasat University.

## Four-year study plan

The plan below sets out a typical route through the programme. Courses shown as changeable in the source handout (marked "coloured and underlined" in the original) can be changed or shuffled between semesters, depending on your own decisions and planning.

### Year 1

**Semester 1**

- TU100 Civic Engagement
- TU101 Thailand, ASEAN and the World
- TU103 Life and Sustainability
- EL105 English Communication Skills
- TU106 Creativity and Communication
- PD102 Social Sciences in the 21st Century

**Semester 2**

- TU102 Social Life Skills
- LAS101 Critical Thinking, Reading and Writing
- PD103 Humanities in the Age of Disruption
- Select 1 course from: AH208 Exercise for Good Health and Well-Being, or EL295 Academic English and Study Skill 1
- PI211 Introduction to Political Science
- PI271 Introduction to International Relations

<Notice variant="info">
TU/PD/EL/LAS courses may differ. This depends on your exemption result and the course arrangement each semester.
</Notice>

- PI241 Introduction to Public Administration and Public Policy
