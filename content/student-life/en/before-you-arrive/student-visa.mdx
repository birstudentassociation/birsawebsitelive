Apply for a Non-Immigrant ED (education) visa from outside Thailand, after BIR has admitted you and you hold your acceptance letter. Use the visa within 90 days of the day it is issued.

## Apply from abroad

Apply online at [thaievisa.go.th](https://www.thaievisa.go.th) through the Thai embassy or consulate that serves your country. You normally need the following.

- A passport valid for at least 6 months
- The visa application form
- A recent photo, 4 by 6 cm
- The acceptance letter from the university
- Your academic records

Each embassy sets its own checklist and fee, so read the one for your country.

The ED visa is single entry. It lets you stay up to 90 days at first. You then extend your stay in Thailand, as described in [visa rules while you study](/student-life/rules-and-rights/visa-rules).

### ED Plus

Some Thai universities can issue an ED Plus visa to degree students. Holders leave and re-enter without a re-entry permit. Ask the BIR office whether Thammasat issues it before you apply.

## Submit the TDAC before you land

Every non-Thai traveller, including children, must submit the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) at [tdac.immigration.go.th](https://tdac.immigration.go.th). Submit it within 3 days before you arrive, counting the day of arrival, which is up to 72 hours. There is no fee and no student exemption. Thailand no longer uses a paper departure card.

## What to carry

Keep these in hand luggage, printed or saved on your phone.

- Your passport with the ED visa
- The immigration approval or acceptance letter you used for the visa
- The TDAC confirmation
- The address of your first accommodation, which the TDAC also asks for

## Visas that do not cover study

Enter on the ED visa. Ask the BIR office before you enter visa-exempt or on a tourist visa and plan to change to an ED visa later. According to secondary sources, the visa-exempt stay for many nationalities is 30 days from 15 September 2026, down from 60. Check the current rule on the Immigration Bureau website.

The Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) is for remote work and similar activities. It is not a study visa, so do not use it for BIR.
