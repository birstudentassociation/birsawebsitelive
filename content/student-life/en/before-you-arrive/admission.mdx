BIR is the Bachelor of Political Science in Politics and International Relations (International Program) at the Faculty of Political Science, Thammasat University. It started in 2009 and is taught in English at Tha Prachan. There are three ways to apply, and the route depends on your nationality and your school results.

<Notice variant="info" title="Admission 2027 dates, checked 30 September 2026">

- Inter-Portfolio 1 runs from 6 October to 6 November 2026.
- Inter-Portfolio 2 runs from 6 to 24 January 2027.
- TCAS 3 applications run from 2 to 17 February 2027. The written exam is on 21 March 2027.

Confirm the dates on [the BIR admission page](https://www.birpolsci.com/prospectivestudents) before you apply.

</Notice>

## Choose a route

| Route              | Who it is for                                 | What you need                                                       |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inter-Portfolio 1  | Applicants who do not hold Thai nationality   | SAT report, no minimum GPAX, committee interview                    |
| Inter-Portfolio 2  | Applicants finishing Mathayom 6 or equivalent | GPAX of at least 3.75, English test, portfolio, committee interview |
| TCAS 3 (Admission) | Thai applicants                               | GPAX of at least 2.80, English test, BIR written exam               |

You must not have confirmed a place through TCAS before you apply.

### International applicants

Applicants without Thai nationality apply in either Inter-Portfolio round. The application fee is 1,000 baht or 35 US dollars and is not refundable. Submit these documents.

- A copy of your passport
- A photo, 4 by 3 cm
- Your transcript
- Your SAT report
- A CV
- A statement of purpose of 500 to 1,000 words

Interviews for round 1 are on 15 November 2026, with results on 15 December 2026. Round 2 interviews are on 23 February 2027, with results on 11 May 2027. Transcripts from outside Thailand need an equivalence certificate, and a GED needs at least 165 in each subject.

## Grade and English test requirements

TCAS 3 applicants need a GPAX of at least 2.80 across Mathayom 4 to 6 (six semesters), or an equivalent qualification.

Every applicant needs an English test result from the last two years.

| Test   | Minimum result                            |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| TOEFL  | 500 paper-based or 61 internet-based      |
| IELTS  | Overall 6.0                               |
| TU-GET | 500 paper-based                           |
| SAT    | 50% on Evidence-Based Reading and Writing |

BIR sources give both 61 and 74 as the TU-GET computer-based score, so confirm the current figure with the BIR office. The Faculty page also lists a GSAT reading and writing score of 400.

## TCAS 3 written exam

The exam is on 21 March 2027. Passing it is a condition of admission, and interviews follow. It has two parts.

- Politics and international relations general knowledge, multiple choice, 40%
- An English essay on politics and international relations, 60%

TCAS confirmation takes place in May 2027. The BIR pages give slightly different end dates, so check the exact days on the BIR site.

## How the programme runs

BIR is full time. Each academic year has two semesters, from August to December and from January to May. The summer session, from June to July, lasts 6 to 8 weeks and is optional, except that the summer after year 3 is the compulsory [internship](/student-life/studying/internship).

All lectures, reading, exams and class participation are in English. Graduates receive the Bachelor of Political Science (Politics and International Relations), shortened to B.Pol.Sc. (Politics and International Relations).
