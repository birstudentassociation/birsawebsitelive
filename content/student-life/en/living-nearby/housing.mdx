## TU Dorm Tha Prachan

The dorm costs about 2,700 baht per person a month in a double room, or about 5,400 baht for the room. The deposit is about 2,000 baht. Rooms are furnished, with air conditioning and Wi-Fi.

Book at [psm.tu.ac.th/registertpc](https://psm.tu.ac.th/registertpc) or on LINE at @baantuofficial. The Office of International Affairs does not manage bookings. Its [accommodation page](https://oia.tu.ac.th/accommodation-page/) lists the details.

## Private housing near campus

## Renting a private room

- The deposit is usually 1 to 2 months of rent. The first month is paid in advance.
- Ask for the price per unit of electricity and water, and whether each room has its own meter. Landlords set these rates, and some charge more than the utility does.
- Ask for the lease length and what it costs to leave early. Get the terms in writing.
- Never pay a deposit before you have seen the room in person.
- Keep a receipt for every payment.

See [What a month costs](/student-life/money/monthly-costs) for the other costs of moving in.

## TM30 for international students

Your landlord must file TM30 with immigration within 24 hours of your arrival at each address. Ask for the receipt. You need it for a visa extension and often for the 90-day report. See [Visa rules while you study](/student-life/rules-and-rights/visa-rules).
