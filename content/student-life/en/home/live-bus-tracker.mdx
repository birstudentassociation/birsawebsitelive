Three public bus stops ring the Tha Prachan campus: **Thammasat University** on the campus side of Phrachan Road, **Opposite Tha Phra Chan** directly across from it, and **Sanam Luang (Opposite Thammasat)** a short walk north. Between them they cover almost every bus you can catch near campus.

The board below is live. For each stop it lists every line that calls there, its next three stops and where the line ends, and how long until the next bus actually arrives. Lines with a bus on the way rise to the top; a line with no tracked bus stays listed with its route so you can still see where it goes.

## Good to know

<Notice variant="warning" title="Live times are estimates">
  Arrival times come from the buses' live GPS positions on Namtang, the Department of Land
  Transport's open-data service. Traffic can throw them off, and a bus running its schedule without
  a working tracker will not appear at all. Treat the numbers as a good guide, not a guarantee, and
  leave a buffer if you are catching a train or sitting an exam.
</Notice>

Fares, air-conditioning and step-free access are shown per line where the operator publishes them. Most routes here are run by the Bangkok Mass Transit Authority or Thai Smile Bus.
