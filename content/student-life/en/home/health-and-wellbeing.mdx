## Everyday health care: the TU Virtual Clinic

The **TU Virtual Clinic** is the campus sick bay, in the health area beneath the student activity building, opposite the gym. It is open Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 16:30. Bring your student card and your ID card (or passport, for international students).

For minor illnesses and injuries, check here before going to a private hospital. If your symptoms are more serious than the clinic can handle, staff there can refer you on to hospital.

## What your student status pays for

As a Thammasat student, you are covered for both routine treatment and accidents.

### Thammasat student healthcare entitlement

Treatment at Thammasat University Hospital (โรงพยาบาลธรรมศาสตร์เฉลิมพระเกียรติ) is covered as follows:

| Cover                                                                    | Amount                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| General treatment                                                        | 5,000 baht per visit, up to 20,000 baht per academic year |
| Basic dental (check up, filling, extraction, scaling, X-ray)             | Up to 300 baht per visit, 3 times per academic year       |
| Basic lab tests (CBC, stool exam, urine exam, plain film X-ray)          | Covered                                                   |
| Drugs on the national essential medicines list, room, and standard meals | Covered                                                   |

Thammasat University Hospital is at the Rangsit campus, not Tha Prachan.

Separately, if you hold the universal coverage "gold card" (บัตรทอง), you can transfer it to Thammasat University Hospital. Either way, the gold card covers diagnosis, treatment and rehabilitation; food and a standard room as an inpatient; drugs and medical supplies on the national essential list; unlimited extractions, fillings and scaling; plastic-base dentures; and health promotion and disease prevention and control. In an accident or emergency you can use any hospital near where it happened, with no limit on which one.

### Accident insurance

Every Thammasat student is covered by student accident insurance. You can check your own cover by showing your ID card and student card to hospital staff.

If you are on the policy at a **contract hospital**, you can direct claim with no need to pay upfront. If you are not yet on the policy, you can be added afterwards. If you are on the policy but use a hospital that is not a contract hospital, you pay upfront yourself and then bring the documents to the Student Affairs office at your centre, who forward them to the insurance company for reimbursement.

The contract hospital for Tha Prachan is **Chao Phraya Pinklao Hospital** (โรงพยาบาลเจ้าพระยาปิ่นเกล้า).

| Cover                                                                                               | Amount       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| Accident treatment (per accident)                                                                   | 15,000 baht  |
| Death, loss of organ/sight/hearing/speech, or permanent disability from accident, assault or murder | 150,000 baht |
| Funeral costs, death from general illness                                                           | 15,000 baht  |

## Mental health and counselling

Routes into support at Tha Prachan:

- **TU Well Being, via the TU Greats App.** Book through the app. Online sessions run Monday to Friday; onsite sessions run Wednesday to Friday, 08:00 to 16:00. Onsite appointments happen on floor 3 of the Student Affairs office, in the student activity building. There is also a 24-hour booking line: 02-026-2345, press 2.
- **Viva City counselling centre.** Weekdays, 08:30 to 16:30, also bookable through the TU Greats App.
- **TCAPS**, the counselling centre at the Faculty of Liberal Arts, at both Tha Prachan and Rangsit.
- **Relationflip**, an online counselling service at [happy.relationflip.com/registerUniversity](https://happy.relationflip.com/registerUniversity). It does not prescribe medication. If you register but do not receive your login details by email, or run into any other issue, contact the RF call centre on 099-002-6888.

If you're supporting a friend who seems to be struggling, encourage them to talk to someone: a counsellor, a trusted staff member, or you.

## When to see a doctor versus wait it out

Minor colds and aches usually resolve with rest. See a doctor promptly if symptoms are severe, do not improve after a few days, or if you're unsure. The TU Virtual Clinic is the first stop.

## Taking care of yourself day to day

- Sleep and regular meals support mood and focus during busy weeks.
- BIRSA and faculty events are a low-pressure way to stay socially connected. See [Getting involved](/student-life/home/getting-involved).
- Check what your student healthcare entitlement or accident insurance covers before paying out of pocket.

## In a crisis

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call 1669 or 191 without delay.
