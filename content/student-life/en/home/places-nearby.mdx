## Tips

- Prices around Tha Prachan are generally student-friendly compared with tourist-facing streets nearby.
- Small market stalls mostly expect cash, so it's worth keeping some small notes and coins on you.
- The Tha Prachan–Wang Lang ferry is a quick, cheap way to reach a lot of the food options across the river.
