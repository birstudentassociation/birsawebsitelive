Tha Prachan sits in one of Bangkok's most walkable, food-dense old-town neighbourhoods, with Pinklao a short ride across the river. This page maps around 70 food places and 16 recommended places to live, collected from Google Maps lists kept and shared by BIRSA seniors.

## Tips

- Small market stalls mostly expect cash. Keep small notes and coins on you.
- The Tha Prachan to Wang Lang ferry is a quick, cheap way to reach food options across the river.
- The buffet and BBQ places around Pinklao suit group dinners after activities.
