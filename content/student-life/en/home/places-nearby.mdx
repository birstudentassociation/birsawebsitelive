Tha Prachan sits in one of Bangkok's most walkable, food-dense old-town neighbourhoods, with Pinklao a short ride across the river. This page maps a food trail of around 70 places and 16 recommended places to live, collected from Google Maps lists that BIRSA seniors have kept and shared over the years.

## Tips

- Small market stalls mostly expect cash, so it's worth keeping some small notes and coins on you.
- The Tha Prachan to Wang Lang ferry is a quick, cheap way to reach a lot of the food options across the river.
- The buffet and BBQ places around Pinklao are a good, affordable choice for group dinners after activities.
