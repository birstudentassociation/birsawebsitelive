## Clubs

Clubs at Thammasat run at three levels: within the BIR programme itself, across Tha Prachan Campus, and university-wide. You can join clubs at any or all of these levels.

### BIR clubs

BIR students run 12 clubs of their own: volunteer camps, a podcast, a writers' club, music, four sports teams including esports, a card game club, and three clubs that simulate an institution (the United Nations, the Thai Parliament, and an investment fund). See [Clubs](/clubs) for the full BIR club directory.

Thammasat also runs university-wide clubs and societies, covering academics, hobbies and service. As a Thammasat student, you can join those too.

## Elected student bodies

Thammasat also elects students to represent you and to hold student organisations accountable.

### TUSU Tha Prachan (อมธ. ท่าพระจันทร์)

The Thammasat University Student Union (TUSU, องค์การนักศึกษามหาวิทยาลัยธรรมศาสตร์ or อมธ.) represents students and is directly elected by them. It is structured in four parts: a central union (อมธ. ส่วนกลาง) alongside separate unions for each campus, Tha Prachan (อมธ. ท่าพระจันทร์), Rangsit (อมธ. ศูนย์รังสิต) and Lampang (อมธ. ศูนย์ลำปาง).

TUSU's role has three parts:

- organising activities and projects that develop student life across the university
- protecting student rights and promoting student welfare
- supporting the work of clubs, activity groups and each faculty's student committee

### TUSC (สภานักศึกษา)

The Thammasat University Student Council (TUSC, สภานักศึกษา) is also directly elected, with 100 members drawn from students across all three campuses (Tha Prachan, Rangsit and Lampang). Its role:

- it speaks for students to the university's administrators on complaints, welfare and campus life issues
- it approves the budgets that TUSU, clubs and activity groups draw from the student activity fund
- it scrutinises how TUSU, the election commission, clubs and activity groups spend that money

## Doing things on campus

**Borrowing sports equipment.** Bring your student card to your own faculty, or to the TUSU Tha Prachan room on floor 2 of the student activity building (ตึกกิจกรรมนักศึกษา).

**Booking a room.** To book a room in the student activity building or the gym, use the [SATU booking system](https://sa.tu.ac.th/oth/SATU_booking/MENU_booking/) and attach the request form (แบบฟอร์มขอความอนุเคราะห์).

## BIRSA events

BIRSA organises events throughout the year: orientation activities, socials, talks, and faculty-wide gatherings. These are open to all BIR students and are usually free or low-cost. Check [What's on](/news) for sign-up windows.

## Volunteering

Volunteering opportunities run through BIRSA, the faculty, and independent causes, ranging from one-off event support to ongoing commitments. Check BIRSA's channels for calls for volunteers, especially around bigger events.

## Benefits of taking part

- Regular club meetings build friendships that last past a single semester.
- Clubs and volunteering build skills, organising, public speaking, teamwork, that do not show up on a transcript.
- As a Thammasat student you vote in three separate elections. Getting involved helps you understand what you are voting for. See [Your rights and welfare](/student-life/home/rights-and-welfare) for what you can vote on.

## Getting started

Turn up to a BIRSA event, or message a club directly.
