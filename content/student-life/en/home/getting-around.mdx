Tha Prachan is BIR's home campus, a compact, historic site right on the Chao Phraya river, inside Bangkok's old city. Once you know the routes, getting to and from class is quick and cheap.

## Reaching campus

Three ways cover almost everyone:

- **River boats**. The Chao Phraya Express Boat stops at Tha Chang and Tha Phra Chan piers, both a short walk from the faculty. Boats are often the fastest way in during rush hour traffic.
- **MRT**. The Blue Line's Sanam Chai station is about a 10 to 15 minute walk away, past the Grand Palace wall. Good if you're coming from the eastern side of the city.
- **Buses**. Several public bus routes stop along Na Phra Lan and Phra Chan roads. Cheapest option, but slower and less predictable than the boat or MRT.

Most students mix all three depending on where they live and the time of day.

## Landmarks around you

Tha Prachan sits inside a cluster of some of Bangkok's most recognisable sites:

- **Sanam Luang**, the large public field just north of campus, used for ceremonies and as a green space to sit and study outdoors.
- **The Grand Palace**, the walled palace complex a short walk south, easy to point to when giving directions to visitors.
- **Wat Phra Kaew and the river piers**, useful orientation points if you're new to the area.

Knowing these names helps when explaining where you are to taxi or ride-hailing drivers, since "Thammasat Tha Prachan" alone does not always register.

## Walking the campus

The campus is small and walkable, but streets in the old city are narrow and pavements can be uneven. Wear shoes you can walk in, and allow extra time in the rainy season when some routes flood briefly.

## Getting further afield

For trips across the city, ride-hailing apps and the BTS/MRT network (reachable by connecting through Sanam Chai or a short taxi ride) cover most destinations. Traffic is heaviest on weekday evenings; the boat is usually still the most reliable way home along the river.

## Tips from students who've done it

- Top up a rabbit card or use a transit app so you're not scrambling for cash at the pier or station.
- If you're unsure which boat flag colour stops where, ask at the pier; staff are used to students asking.
- Google Maps transit directions are generally reliable for Bangkok's boats, MRT and buses together.
