Thammasat runs two free shuttle bus lines from the Tha Prachan campus, Monday to Friday. One is a quick hop to the MRT; the other loops across the river to the Pinklao area and back. Both are boarded in front of the Thammasat University Auditorium (หน้าหอประชุมมหาวิทยาลัยธรรมศาสตร์).

## Routes

The **Sanam Chai Line** is a straight shuttle between campus and the MRT, useful if you're heading onto the Blue Line. The **Pinklao Line** is a loop: it heads out to the Pinklao area, makes a full circuit of stops there, then comes back to campus. Two of its rounds each day also double as dormitory shuttles.

## Good to know

<Notice variant="warning" title="Times are scheduled, not guaranteed">
  During peak hours, a bus can fall behind because of traffic. A missed departure is pushed back to
  the next scheduled slot rather than skipped. The printed times and the live countdown above are
  the schedule the buses aim for, not a live position. Build in a buffer if you're catching a train
  or exam.
</Notice>

- You can track the buses live in the **Viabus** app, which shows real-time positions rather than just the schedule.
- There's no shuttle service on Saturdays or Sundays. Plan weekend trips around the boat, MRT, or ride-hailing instead.
- Both lines start and finish at the same boarding point in front of the Auditorium. If you're unsure which bus you're looking at, ask there.
