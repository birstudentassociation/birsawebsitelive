## Dress and titles

- You may wear your own clothes to class and exams, if they do not disturb other people's concentration or rights.
- You may wear the student uniform that matches your gender identity for classes, exams, placements, university business, ceremonies and faculty events.
- You may have your photograph taken in the uniform that matches your gender identity.
- If you graduate, you may wear academic dress and submit graduation photographs according to your gender identity.
- You do not have to use a gendered title. The university uses one only when the law requires it or at new student registration. No title appears on your student card.

## Free menstrual products and condoms

At Tha Prachan, menstrual products are free from your own faculty or from the TUSU Tha Prachan room. A condom dispenser is on floor 1 of the Student Activities Building (ตึกกิจกรรมนักศึกษา).

## Prayer rooms

Tha Prachan has three prayer rooms.

- In front of the gymnasium.
- On floor 6 of the Faculty of Liberal Arts.
- At U1 of Pridi Banomyong Library.

## Booking a room

To book a room in the Student Activities Building or the gymnasium, use the [SATU booking system](https://sa.tu.ac.th/oth/SATU_booking/MENU_booking/) and attach the request form (แบบฟอร์มขอความอนุเคราะห์).

## Sports equipment

Bring your student card to your own faculty or to the TUSU Tha Prachan room on floor 2 of the Student Activities Building.

## The TU Greats app

TU Greats is the university app. It holds your digital student card and lets you book services such as counselling appointments. Install it from your phone's app store. See [Your first two weeks](/student-life/first-weeks/first-two-weeks) for setting up your account.

## Parking

Undergraduates may bring a car onto campus only from 16:30. See [Getting to Tha Prachan](/student-life/getting-around/getting-to-campus) for parking.
