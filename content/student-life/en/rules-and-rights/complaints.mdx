## Reporting harassment or bullying

Report harassment or bullying by another student, a staff member or a member of the public through either channel below. You can ask for advice before you decide to report.

<ReportHarassment />

BIRSA takes reports through its Rights Advocate and Student Welfare Officer. Both channels say they process reports within 48 hours and keep them confidential. For support afterwards, see [Counselling and mental health](/student-life/health-and-safety/mental-health).

## Free legal advice

The TU Law Center at Tha Prachan gives free legal advice to students who have experienced assault, harassment or a rights violation. Call 02-613-2128.

## Other complaints

- Registration goes to your faculty and the university Registrar's office.
- Course content, fees or your own programme goes first to your faculty student committee. For BIR, that is BIRSA. Rules differ between faculties.
- Other matters can go to TUSU Tha Prachan.

Harassment and bullying do not go through these routes. Use the channels above.

## If it is an emergency

Call 1669 for an ambulance or 191 for the police. See the [Emergency page](/emergency).
