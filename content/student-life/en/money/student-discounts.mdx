## Discounts

The details come from the 2025 student union handbook. Shops change their offers, so ask before you order.

| Place                                                  | Type       | Discount                      | Condition                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Thammasat Book Centre (ศูนย์หนังสือธรรมศาสตร์)         | Bookshop   | 10 percent                    | Spend 100 baht or more                                        |
| Amazon at the Faculty of Law (Amazon @คณะนิติศาสตร์)   | Café       | 10 percent                    | Students and staff                                            |
| Krua Muan (ร้านครัวม่วน), Trok Phra Chan Klang         | Restaurant | 10 percent                    | Students                                                      |
| LUA Café                                               | Café       | 10 percent                    | Show your student card                                        |
| Chang Khua (ช่างคั่ว), Tha Prachan branch              | Café       | 10 percent                    | Show your student card                                        |
| Theatre Riverside (ร้านอาหารเธียเตอร์ริเวอร์ไซด์)      | Restaurant | 10 percent on food and drinks | Show your student card. Unlimited uses. Not with other offers |
| KRAFT CAFE                                             | Café       | 50 baht off food and drinks   | Show your student card. Unlimited uses. Not with other offers |
| National Museum Bangkok (พิพิธภัณฑสถานแห่งชาติ พระนคร) | Museum     | Free entry                    | Show your student card                                        |
| National Gallery (พิพิธภัณฑสถานแห่งชาติ หอศิลป์)       | Museum     | Free entry                    | Show your student card                                        |
| Nitas Rattanakosin (นิทรรศน์รัตนโกสินทร์)              | Museum     | Free entry                    | Show your student card                                        |
| Museum Pier (ท่าพิพิธภัณฑ์)                            | Pier       | 50 percent off                | Show your student card                                        |

"Amazon" here is the Thai café chain, not the online shop.

The museum offers were reported for Thai students. International students should check the museum's entry rules at the ticket desk.

Free printing is available at Pridi Banomyong Library and Puey Ungphakorn Library, both on the Tha Prachan campus. See [Libraries, printing and English tests](/student-life/studying/libraries-and-study-support).

## Part-time work

- International students cannot work without a work permit. See [Visa rules while you study](/student-life/rules-and-rights/visa-rules).
- For a university job, such as research assistance, ask the BIR office about the arrangements.
