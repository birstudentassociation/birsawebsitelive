## Student loans for Thai nationals

The Student Loan Fund (กองทุนเงินให้กู้ยืมเพื่อการศึกษา, กยศ.) lends to Thai nationals only. International students cannot borrow from it.

One type of loan is for students whose family income is 360,000 baht a year or less. The fund sets the loan types, amounts and calendar each academic year. Read the current rules and apply at [studentloan.or.th](https://www.studentloan.or.th). Ask the BIR office which documents the university needs from you.

<Notice variant="info" title="Academic year 2569 (2026 to 2027)">
  The fund's window for new borrowers in academic year 2569 closed on 31 August 2026. Check
  studentloan.or.th for the dates for the next year.
</Notice>

## Thammasat scholarships

The Student Affairs Division runs the university scholarships. Announcements are on Facebook at [facebook.com/thammasat.scholarships](https://www.facebook.com/thammasat.scholarships). The BIR office can also point you to scholarships for BIR students.

International students can also ask the Office of International Affairs at [oia.tu.ac.th](https://oia.tu.ac.th).

## Surin Pitsuwan Scholarship

This scholarship is for applicants from ASEAN countries who pass BIR admission. Applicants must meet these conditions.

- Nationality of an ASEAN country
- A high school GPA of at least 3.5, or the equivalent
- Attendance at an interview
- No other Thammasat scholarship

Applications go to <Email address="bir@tu.ac.th" /> with the application form, a copy of your high school transcript and a copy of your ID card or passport. The amount is not published.

<Notice variant="info" title="2026 round">
  Applications for the 2026 round ran from 23 June to 3 July 2026. Winners were announced on 23 July
  2026. Check [birpolsci.com/scholarship](https://www.birpolsci.com/scholarship) for the next round.
</Notice>

## If you cannot pay on time

Contact the BIR office before the payment deadline. Say how much you can pay and when. The office decides deferrals case by case.

<Email address="bir@tu.ac.th" />, 02-221-6111 ext. 3409.

The office can also tell you about the refund process. See [Tuition, fees and refunds](/student-life/money/tuition-and-fees).
