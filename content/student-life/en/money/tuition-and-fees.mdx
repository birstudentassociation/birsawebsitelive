## What tuition costs

The cost per academic year is about 125,000 baht for Thai students and 144,400 baht for non-Thai students.

## Fee items

The faculty's fee announcement sets these items.

| Item           | Amount in baht | Charged       |
| -------------- | -------------- | ------------- |
| Enrolment fee  | 400            | Once          |
| Tuition        | 2,500          | Per credit    |
| Programme fee  | 12,000         | Each semester |
| Health fee     | 125            | Each semester |
| Sport fee      | 200            | Each semester |
| Activities fee | 200            | Each semester |
| Library fee    | 2,000          | Each year     |

## Paying

Registration payments are made through the TU Greats app, using your TU username. The registration announcement for each semester sets the payment deadline. See [Registering, adding and dropping courses](/student-life/studying/registration).

If you cannot pay by the deadline, see [Deferring payment](#deferring-payment).

## Refunds

You can claim a refund if you dropped a course and did not register for another course in its place.

1. Download the refund request form from the [BIR tuition refund page](https://www.birpolsci.com/content/18918).
2. Take a screenshot from TU REG that shows the course was dropped.
3. Email both files to <Email address="bir@tu.ac.th" /> with the subject "Tuition Fee Refund Request" and the semester. Give your name, student ID and phone number.

The request must reach the BIR office within 7 days after you drop the course in TU REG. The office does not accept late requests.

The student card is also a Bangkok Bank card, and refunds are paid to the linked account once it is activated. See [Opening a bank account](/student-life/money/bank-account).

## W grades and refunds

- A course dropped in the first 14 days of a regular semester, or the first 7 days of summer, leaves no record.
- A course withdrawn after that, up to the end of week 10 of a regular semester or week 4 of summer, shows a W. A W does not affect your GPA. It does not qualify for a refund.
- A later withdrawal needs the Dean's approval.

## Deferring payment

Ask the BIR office, which decides deferrals case by case. Other sources of money are in [Scholarships, loans and hardship help](/student-life/money/financial-help).
