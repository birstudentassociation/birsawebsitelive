## Call now

Call 1323, the Department of Mental Health hotline. It is free and answers 24 hours a day. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 1669 for an ambulance or 191 for the police.

## Counselling services

| Service                      | How to reach it                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| TU Well Being line           | 02-026-2345 ext. 2, open 24 hours. You can also book through the TU Greats app.                                                                              |
| Viva City counselling centre | In person Monday to Friday, 08:30 to 22:00. A call centre answers 24 hours a day. English counselling is available. Check its location with Student Affairs. |
| TCAPS                        | Counselling centre at the Faculty of Liberal Arts.                                                                                                           |
| Relationflip                 | Online counselling. Register at https://happy.relationflip.com. It does not prescribe medication.                                                            |

Viva City is free for Thammasat students. If you register with Relationflip and do not receive your login by email, call the Relationflip call centre on 099-002-6888.

## Supporting a friend

Tell your friend that these services exist and offer to go with them.

## If the problem is harassment or bullying

Counselling does not report an incident. To report harassment or bullying, see [Complaints and reporting harassment](/student-life/rules-and-rights/complaints).
