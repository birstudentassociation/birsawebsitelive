Thammasat runs two free shuttle lines from Tha Prachan, Monday to Friday. Both leave from in front of the Thammasat University Auditorium (หน้าหอประชุมมหาวิทยาลัยธรรมศาสตร์). There is no service on Saturdays or Sundays.

## Where it goes

The Sanam Chai Line runs between campus and the Museum Siam exit of MRT Sanam Chai. The Pinklao Line crosses the river to Pinklao, calls at each stop there and returns to campus. Some Pinklao Line rounds also serve as dormitory shuttles.

## Delays and live positions

<Notice variant="warning" title="Times are scheduled, not guaranteed">
  In heavy traffic a bus can run late. A late departure moves to the next scheduled slot and is not
  cancelled. The timetable and the countdown show the schedule, not where the bus is. Leave a buffer
  if you have a train to catch or an exam to sit.
</Notice>

The Viabus app shows live bus positions. Its free version is limited. Public buses are on [Live bus times](/student-life/getting-around/live-bus-tracker). At weekends use the boat, the MRT or ride-hailing, as described in [Getting to Tha Prachan](/student-life/getting-around/getting-to-campus).
