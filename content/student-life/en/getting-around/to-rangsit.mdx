BIR classes are at Tha Prachan. You may need to go to Rangsit for university offices, events or a course held there.

## Five routes

| Route                 | Steps                                                                                                                                  | Fare                                     | Time                                              |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Expressway bus 1-9E   | Direct from the Sanam Luang stop opposite Tha Prachan to Rangsit                                                                       | 27 baht                                  | About 1 hour 30 minutes                           |
| MRT then van          | MRT Sanam Chai to Chatuchak Park, then the Mo Chit to Thammasat Rangsit van from exit 4                                                | MRT 44 baht, van fare not confirmed      | About 30 minutes by MRT, then about 1 hour by van |
| MRT then SRT Red Line | MRT Sanam Chai to Bang Sue, walk about 5 minutes to Krung Thep Aphiwat, Red Line to Rangsit, then a taxi or ride-hailing car to campus | MRT 44 baht, Red Line fare not confirmed | About 30 minutes, 25 minutes, then 20 minutes     |
| Bus then van          | Bus 1-8 (59) from Sanam Luang to Victory Monument, then the Victory Monument to Thammasat Rangsit van from Koh Phaya Thai              | Bus and van fares not confirmed          | About 1 hour by bus, then about 40 minutes by van |
| Bus then bus          | Bus 1-8 (59) from Sanam Luang to Victory Monument, then bus 1-19 (510) from Koh Phahonyothin to Thammasat Rangsit                      | Bus fares not confirmed                  | About 1 hour by bus, then about 1 hour 30 minutes |

Check current fares at the stop or in the Namtang app. Journey times depend on traffic and can be longer at peak hours. Route 1-9E is direct and slow. MRT then the Red Line is usually fastest and needs the most changes.

MRT Blue Line fares are 17 to 44 baht since 3 July 2026. Pay with a contactless bank card or a Mangmoom card. Old MRT cards stopped working on 1 June 2026, and a Rabbit card works on the BTS only.
