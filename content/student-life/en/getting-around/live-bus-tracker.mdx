## How accurate the times are

<Notice variant="warning" title="Live times are estimates">
  Times come from live bus positions on Namtang, the open transport data service of the Office of
  Transport and Traffic Policy and Planning (OTP, สนข.). Traffic can change them. A bus without a
  working tracker does not appear. Leave a buffer if you have a train to catch or an exam to sit.
</Notice>

Most routes are run by the Bangkok Mass Transit Authority or Thai Smile Bus. Route numbers and fares are in [Getting to Tha Prachan](/student-life/getting-around/getting-to-campus). The free university shuttle is on [Free shuttle bus](/student-life/getting-around/shuttle-bus).
