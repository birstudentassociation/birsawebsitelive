# Fact-check: BIR handbook and international guides

Checked 30 September 2026. Scope: `content/student-life/en/handbook/*.mdx`, `content/student-life/en/international/*.mdx`, `content/onboarding/international.ts`. Read-only; no repo files edited.

Verdict key: OK = correct; OUTDATED = was right, superseded; WRONG = contradicted by an authoritative source; CONFLICT = authoritative sources disagree with each other; UNVERIFIED = no authoritative source found (reason given).

## 0. Sources used and their limits

Authoritative (fetched and read):

- S1 birpolsci.com pages (aboutbir, prospectivestudents, tcas3admission, trackoutside, contact-us, scholarship, partnerships, incomingstudents, studentservices, frequentlyaskedquestions, birinternship, /content/18918 tuition refund, /content/21146 Surin Pitsuwan 2026), all retrieved via the fetch tool. `curl` gets HTTP 403 from birpolsci.com (bot block), so a browser check is still worth doing.
- S2 Faculty of Political Science site, fetched with curl: https://polsci.tu.ac.th/en/bir-eng/ (information as of 8 April 2025), /en/history-eng/, /en/contact-us-2/, /en/exchange-students/.
- S3 BIR brochure 2025: https://image.makewebcdn.com/makeweb/0/fAusajSlU/Document/Brochure2025.pdf
- S4 TU Undergraduate Studies Regulation B.E. 2561 (2018), English translation, https://www.tbs.tu.ac.th/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Thammasat-University-Undergraduate-Studies-Regulation-2561-B.E..pdf. **Repealed** by S5.
- S5 **TU Regulation on Undergraduate Studies B.E. 2568 (2025)**, in force from 9 June 2025 and repealing both the 2561 regulation and its 2nd edition of 2564. Thai text at https://db.legal.tu.ac.th/05010201-01/ (PDF and DOC linked there). I read Articles 11, 20, 24, 27, 28, 49 to 51, 65, 67 to 69 directly from the scanned PDF and DOC. Article numbers below are 2568 unless marked "2561". The DOC text extraction dropped some Latin text, so treat anything I have not quoted as unchecked.
- S6 TU Office of International Affairs (OIA) Fact Sheet for International Students, updated 5 August 2025 (https://en.apu.ac.jp/abroad/university/infosheet/files/Thammasat_University.pdf), plus https://oia.tu.ac.th/ and /well-being-services/ and /accommodation-page/ (fetch tool).
- S7 Thai Embassy consular pages (MFA) and TDAC FAQ https://tdac.immigration.go.th/manual/en/faq.html.

Not reachable, so second-hand only: immigration.go.th and bangkok.immigration.go.th (403 to the fetch tool; the search snippets give the fee pages), nbtc.go.th, the Bangkok Bank, Kasikorn and SCB pages (503 or no relevant text), reg.tu.ac.th (homepage only, no calendar). For visa fees, 90-day rules, TM30, SIM and bank practice I had to rely on law-firm and news sites, which I flag as "secondary". Treat those as needing a check against immigration.go.th before publishing.

## 7. Bigger issues to raise

1. The site cites TU regulations that are no longer in force (1997, 2012 3rd edition). The current instrument is the B.E. 2568 Regulation (9 June 2025). It changed the credit ceiling (22), removed the stated attendance percentage, changed the graduation-request window (10 days), and may have dropped the 9-credit minimum and minimum-semester rule. Whoever owns academic-life and assessment-and-degree should re-read Arts. 11, 20, 24, 27 to 28, 45, 49 to 53, 65 to 69 of the 2568 regulation. Programme-specific rules (attendance, honours announcement, 7 semesters) may still be set by the Faculty or the curriculum specification, so ask BIR which apply.
2. Credit total is cohort-dependent (127 vs 126); the two handbook chapters state 127 without saying so.
3. Contact details on about-bir.mdx are wrong (phone, email); the exchange email and link are wrong.
4. Visa page omits TDAC, TM30, ED Plus, fees, work restrictions, and mentions a departure card that no longer exists.
5. Banking claims, SIM rules and fees rest on secondary sources for now.
