## How to read this

- Verdicts: **correct**, **outdated**, **wrong**, **unverifiable** (could not reach an authoritative page), **inconsistent** (conflicts with another page in this repo or with the source).
- Confidence tags: **[P]** = I read the primary page myself (curl or fetch). **[S]** = secondary or news source. **[Repo]** = checked against repo data, not the outside world.
- Several university sites were unreachable from this sandbox (proxy denied or DNS failure): sa.tu.ac.th (frameset that redirects to satu.colorpack.net, which returned 503), sdgs.tu.ac.th, userservice.library.tu.ac.th and studentloan.or.th (search snippets only). Anything that depends on those is marked unverifiable rather than guessed.
- The guides say they draw on the TU91 Handbook (2025). I did not read the flipbook, so "unverifiable" can mean "probably right per handbook, but no live source found".
